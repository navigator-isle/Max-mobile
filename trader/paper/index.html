The rules of each strategy
  • Crypto swing: Binance top 20 coins by 30-day traded value. Buy when a coin closes above its 20-day high, but only while Bitcoin is above its 50-day average (otherwise wait in cash). Stop 2×ATR below, trailing up from the highest close. Risk 0.5% of the book per trade, at most 8 coins, at most 25% in one. Fee and slippage 0.15% each side.
  • Bitcoin trend: hold Bitcoin only while it closes above its 100-day average, sized so its swings stay near 40% a year (at most 100% of the book); otherwise cash. 30% of the crypto money (9% of the total).
  • PSE high-dividend top 20: the 20 stocks with the highest cash dividends of the last 12 months ÷ price (at least 3%), at least ₱5M traded a day, price at least ₱1. Equal pesos, whole board lots, rebalanced every 3 months; a holding is kept while it stays in the top 40, so it can still pay its dividend. Safety stop: sell if a stock closes 25% below its highest close since buying (cash waits for the next rebalance). COL fees (buy 0.295%, sell 0.395%) plus 0.2% slippage. Dividends credited on the ex-date after 10% tax.
  • Forex fade big traders: every CFTC report, for 10 currencies vs USD, go against the leveraged funds: if they raised their net position above the 13-week average, go short; if they cut it, go long. Equal size, no leverage.
  • Copy steady traders: each month copy the 10 Hyperliquid traders with the best 6-month consistency (at least 4 of 6 green months, last 90 days positive, account at least $100k). 0.5% a month taken off for copy delay.
  • Watch only: crypto momentum rotation (top 5 by 14-day return when BTC is above its 50-day average) and copy hot traders (top 10 by last 30 days). Both lost money in testing.
  • MAX-MASTER-2: 21% crypto swing, 9% Bitcoin trend, 30% PSE dividend, 25% forex, 15% copy steady. Backtest 2022 to Oct 2026: +85% (13.6%/yr), worst drop −7.2%.
