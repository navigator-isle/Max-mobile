**MAX-MASTER-1 — all strategy types × crypto, PSE, forex (8 Nov 2026)**
Built by Claude acting as Max's Trading agent. Paper money only — not financial advice. Past results don't promise future results.

**Data (widest reachable)**
- Crypto: 122 coins with price + market cap (Coin Metrics), incl. coins that later collapsed; point-in-time top-20 by market cap (80 different coins were top-20 at some point) · 2016 → Nov 2026 · BTC 1-min for day trading
- PSE: all 318 stocks with history (2009 → Mar 2021) · 21 blue chips daily Jun → Nov 2026 · 30-min feed since Sep 2026 · PSE Edge dividend list
- Forex: 21 currencies vs USD daily (Fed H.10, 2000 → Oct 2026) · 4 majors + gold hourly/daily (2012 → 2022) · EURUSD 15-min 2026
- Copy trading: CFTC Commitments of Traders — weekly positions of leveraged funds (hedge funds/big speculators) in 10 currencies, CME Bitcoin & Ether, 2018 → 3 Nov 2026

**How tested**
- Settings chosen only on older years; judged on unseen years · trade at the next close · real costs (crypto 0.15%/side, PSE 0.75%/side all stocks, FX 0.02%)
- Combination rule fixed BEFORE looking at unseen years: keep a strategy only if older-years Sharpe ≥ 0.7 AND its worst drop was smaller than just holding

**Results by type (unseen years: crypto 2022→Nov 2026 · PSE 2017→Mar 2021 · forex 2018→Oct 2026)**

*Crypto (benchmark, hold top-20 equally: −14%, worst drop −70%)*
- Regular / trend (top-20 above 20-day average): +25%, drop −54%
- Regular / momentum rotation (best 5 of top-20 by 14-day return, weekly, BTC > 50-day avg): **+319%**, drop −61% (biggest gains, biggest swings)
- Swing / breakout + 2×ATR trailing stop (top-20): **+122%**, drop −29% (best balance)
- Swing / RSI(2) pullback: +12%, drop −8% (steady, small)
- Day trading (BTC opening-range breakout): −38% with normal fees; +21% only if fees ≤ 0.10% round trip
- Copy big traders (CME Bitcoin, fade leveraged funds): +52%, drop −42% · (CME Ether: −42%)
- Dividend: not applicable to crypto

*PSE (benchmark, hold all stocks: −20%, worst drop −53%)*
- Regular / momentum rotation (10 strongest stocks by 6-month return, monthly, PSEi > 200-day avg): **+45%**, drop −27% — the only PSE edge found
- Regular / trend: −23% · Swing breakout: −16% · RSI(2)/Bollinger swing: −15% / −24%
- Day trading: −74% after ~0.9% round-trip costs (6 weeks of 30-min data)
- Dividend trading (2026 events SMC, ALI): run-up −2.1% / −0.8%, capture −0.8% / −1.0% → costs + 10% tax eat the dividend · holding dividend payers for income (TEL ~₱46–50, GLO ₱25 per quarter) is investing, not trading
- Copy big traders: no PSE positioning data exists (foreign-flow data not reachable)

*Forex (no "hold" benchmark)*
- Regular / trend: −14% · momentum rotation: −13% · swing breakout: −14%
- Carry basket (AUD, NZD, MXN, BRL, ZAR, INR vs JPY, CHF; price only): −7% (real carry also earns the interest gap, ~3–6%/yr — interest-rate data not reachable)
- Day trading (London breakout / Asian fade): ≈ break-even
- Copy big traders (10 currencies, fade leveraged funds): +5%, drop −9% — the only forex edge, small

**Plain verdict**
- Solid edge: crypto trend/momentum/breakout with trailing stops; PSE momentum rotation
- Weak/none: forex (except small COT fade), dividend trading, day trading with normal fees
- MAX-MASTER-1 trades lower returns for much smaller drops (−20% vs −61% for the best single crypto part). Higher returns = accept bigger drops (crypto swing alone: +122%, −29%)
