# RO Code — Code for Recognized Organizations

## At a glance
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Adopted | 21 Jun 2013, MSC.349(92) · 17 May 2013, MEPC.237(65) (identical text) |
| In force | 1 Jan 2015, with the related SOLAS, 1988 Load Lines Protocol and MARPOL amendments |
| Mandatory parts | Parts 1 and 2, under SOLAS, the Load Lines Protocol and MARPOL Annexes I and II |
| Non-mandatory | Part 3 (oversight): recommended as a basis for national standards |
| Structure | Preamble · Part 1 General (6) · Part 2 Requirements (8) · Part 3 Oversight (7) · Appendices 1–3 |
| Who | Recognized Organizations (ROs) acting for flag States, and the flag States that authorize them |

![[diagram:ro-structure|Structure of the RO Code]]

> **Key Point:** The flag State may delegate, but must not authorize functions beyond the RO's capabilities. Oversight stays with the flag State.

## What the Code does
- The international standard and consolidated instrument of **minimum criteria for recognition and authorization** of ROs, plus guidelines for flag State oversight (Part 1, section 1)
- Covers ROs seeking or holding recognition, and flag States that intend to recognize ROs (2.1)
- Mandatory requirements for ROs and flag States (2.2); guidance for oversight (2.2.4)

## Part 1 — General
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 Purpose | Minimum criteria and consolidated standard |
| 2 Scope | ROs and flag States |
| 3 Contents | The three-part layout |
| 4 Delegation of authority | A flag State may delegate authority to a recognized RO (4.1) · must not authorize functions beyond the RO's capabilities (4.2) · should cooperate with other flag States (4.3) |
| 5 Communication of information | The flag State deposits its list of ROs and their delegated responsibilities with the IMO Secretary-General |
| 6 References | IMO instruments, ISO 9000/9001, ISO/IEC 17020, ISO 19011, IACS QMSR |

## Part 2 — Recognition and authorization requirements (mandatory)
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 Terms | RO, authorization, statutory certification and services, assessment, interested parties, location, site, Vertical Contract Audit (VCA) |
| 2 General requirements | 2.2 Rules published and maintained, with an English version · 2.3 **Independence**: no conflicts with judgement, no substantial dependence on a single commercial client · 2.4 Impartiality · 2.5 Integrity, under a Code of Ethics · 2.6 Competence · 2.7 Responsibility · 2.8 Transparency, including flag State reporting |
| 3 Management and organization | Quality management system (QMS) · quality, safety and pollution prevention policy · quality manual · document and record control · designated management representative · communication, including cooperation between ROs on ship transfers · **management review at intervals of no more than 13 months** (3.10) |
| 4 Resources | Personnel (4.2) · infrastructure · safe work environment |
| 5 Statutory certification and services processes | Design and development of services · subcontracting (5.9) · calibration of monitoring equipment (5.10) · complaints (5.11) · appeals (5.12) |
| 6 Performance measurement | Internal audits, **each location at least once every 3 years** (6.2) · annual Vertical Contract Audits (6.3) · non-conformity control · continual improvement |
| 7 QMS certification | QMS based on the ISO 9000 series, periodically certified by an accredited body (ISO/IEC 17021:2006). IMO monitors this process (7.3). |
| 8 Authorization of ROs | See below |

![[diagram:ro-cycles|Audit and review intervals]]

### Personnel (4.2)
- Statutory surveys and services are performed only by full-time surveyors and auditors **employed exclusively by the RO** (4.2.4). Radio survey work may be subcontracted under conditions.
- Qualified personnel with adequate geographic coverage and local representation as needed (4.2.3)
- Minimum education (4.2.5): a university degree in a relevant field (at least 2 years), or maritime training with sea experience as a certified officer, plus good English
- A training-tracking system (4.2.7)

### Vertical Contract Audits (6.3)
Annual, for plan approval, new construction, in-service surveys, and type approval or equipment survey.

### Authorization (section 8)
- Applies only to ships entitled to fly the flag State's flag (8.1)
- 8.2 Legal basis: written agreement, laws, interpretations and deviations
- 8.3 Specification: ship types, conventions, drawings, surveys, certificates, corrective actions, reporting
- 8.4 Resources · 8.5 Instruments · 8.6 Instructions · 8.7 Records

## Part 3 — Oversight of recognized organizations (non-mandatory)
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Purpose and scope: guidance for flag State inspection, auditing and monitoring |
| 4 Terms | **Oversight** is any flag State activity that assures RO compliance. **Monitoring** is a component of oversight: witnessing services or reviewing documentation. |
| 5 Establishing an oversight programme | 5.1 Supplementary surveys and qualified staff · 5.2 Supervision of delegated duties (QMS documentation, instructions, access to records, statistics) · 5.3 Verification and monitoring, including reporting obligations (such as notifying the flag State of major deficiencies) and certificate transfer cooperation |
| 6 Principles of auditing | Ethical conduct, fair presentation, due professional care, independence, evidence-based approach |
| 7 Managing an oversight programme | Verify RO compliance, taking into account factors such as remote monitoring and port State control results (7.1) · objectives and extent · responsibilities, resources, procedures · monitoring and review with performance indicators (7.4) |

## Not included (not verified against the original text)
- Old note's structure of "Part 3 oversight" mandatory and "Part 4 model agreement". The Code has **three** parts plus three appendices, and the agreement elements are Appendix 3.
- The SOLAS regulation number (the text says "SOLAS", the Load Lines Protocol and MARPOL Annexes I and II)
- Earlier resolutions A.739(18) and A.789(19) as predecessors
- List of IACS member societies
- Port State control "RO-related detention" ratings and MoU performance tables
- IACS PR 1A/1B transfer-of-class procedures
- Statutory work list beyond the Appendix 2 certificates named above (ISPS, MLC, AFS and BWM delegation)
- Later amendments to the Code (not checked)
