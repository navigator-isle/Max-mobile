**RO Code — Code for Recognized Organizations**

**What is the RO Code**
- International standard for organizations (mostly classification societies) that do statutory surveys and issue certificates on behalf of a flag State
- Sets minimum criteria to be recognized, how the flag delegates authority and how it oversees the RO
- Mandatory through SOLAS XI-1/1, MARPOL Annex I and II, Load Lines Protocol 1988

**History**
- Before: Guidelines for authorization of organizations acting on behalf of the Administration A.739(18) (1993) and specifications A.789(19) (1995)
- RO Code adopted 21 Jun 2013 by MSC.349(92) and 17 May 2013 by MEPC.237(65)
- Mandatory from 1 Jan 2015 (SOLAS, MARPOL I and II, LL Protocol amendments)

**Applies to**
- Flag States (Administrations) that delegate statutory work
- Recognized Organizations: e.g., IACS members (ABS, BV, ClassNK, DNV, KR, LR, RINA, CCS, IRS, PRS, CRS, TL) and non-IACS ROs
- Statutory work: surveys, audits and certificates under SOLAS, MARPOL, LL, Tonnage, ISM, ISPS (as an RSO), MLC (if delegated), AFS, BWM

---

**Part 1 — General**
- Definitions (Administration, RO, statutory certification and services, delegation, oversight)
- Objective: uniform, high-quality statutory work on behalf of flags

**Part 2 — Requirements for recognized organizations**
- Organization and management: legal entity, adequate resources, documented quality management system (ISO 9001-based and IACS QSCS-type)
- Technical capability: rules, technical staff, research, global network of exclusive surveyors
- Independence and impartiality: no conflict of interest; not controlled by owners/builders it surveys
- Qualification and training of surveyors (records, competence for each task)
- Rules and procedures published and kept up to date
- Internal audits and corrective actions
- Information to the flag: reports of surveys, deficiencies, withdrawal of class, detentions
- Agreement with the flag: written agreement covering scope of delegation, reporting, legal basis (model agreement in Part 3)

**Part 3 — Oversight by the flag State**
- Written agreement, defined scope of authority
- Monitoring: audits of the RO's offices, ships surveyed (flag-state inspections), reviews of PSC detentions with RO responsibility
- Corrective actions and, if needed, withdrawal of recognition
- Flag keeps final responsibility for the completeness and efficiency of surveys (SOLAS I/6)

**Part 4 — Model agreement between Administration and RO** (non-mandatory form)

**RO responsibility and PSC**
- If a ship is detained for a deficiency the RO should have found, PSC records it as "RO-related" — affects the RO's performance list (Paris/Tokyo MoU RO performance tables)

**Key Point**
- Class (RO) issues statutory certificates only within the flag's written delegation — check the certificate states "under authority of the Government of …"
- Flag still responsible; RO-related detentions damage the RO's rating
- When changing flag or class, keep RO survey status continuous (transfer of class agreements, IACS PR 1A/1B)
