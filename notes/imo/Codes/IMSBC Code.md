**IMSBC Code — International Maritime Solid Bulk Cargoes Code**

**What is the IMSBC Code**
- IMO code for the safe stowage and shipment of solid bulk cargoes (excluding grain)
- Mandatory under SOLAS Ch VI (Reg 1-1) and Ch VII Part A-1 (dangerous goods in solid form in bulk)
- Core: shipper's cargo information, test certificates, Individual Schedules per cargo (BCSN)
- Updated every 2 years (like IMDG)

**History**
- Replaced the BC Code (1965, voluntary)
- Adopted 4 Dec 2008 by MSC.268(85) · voluntary from 1 Jan 2009 · mandatory from 1 Jan 2011
- Amdt 07-23 (MSC.539(107)): mandatory 1 Jan 2025
- Amdt 08-25 (MSC.575(110), June 2025): voluntary 1 Jan 2026 · mandatory 1 Jan 2027
- Amdt 09-27: in preparation (CCC sub-committee)

**Applies to**
- All ships to which SOLAS applies carrying solid bulk cargoes (other than grain — see Grain Code)
- Shippers, terminals and competent authorities (testing, certification, approvals)

**Cargo groups**
- Group A: may liquefy if shipped above its Transportable Moisture Limit (TML) — e.g., iron ore fines, nickel ore, bauxite (some), mineral concentrates
- Group B: chemical hazard (may be MHB — Materials Hazardous only in Bulk, or IMDG class) — e.g., coal (self-heating, methane), DRI, sulphur, ammonium nitrate fertilizers
- Group C: neither liquefies nor chemical hazard — e.g., iron ore (lump), limestone, sand
- A cargo can be both A and B

---

**Sections**
- Sec 1 General provisions and definitions (BCSN, TML, FMP, angle of repose, MHB, competent authority)
- Sec 2 General loading, carriage and unloading precautions:
  - Distribute cargo to avoid excessive stress (loading/unloading plan agreed by master and terminal — SOLAS VI/7, BLU Code)
  - Trimming to minimize shift · hatch covers weathertight
- Sec 3 Safety of personnel and ship:
  - Entry into enclosed spaces · fumigation in transit · gas detection and oxygen meters on board
- Sec 4 Assessment of acceptability of consignments:
  - Shipper gives cargo information before loading (BCSN, group, IMDG class, total quantity, moisture content, TML, angle of repose, chemical properties)
  - Certificate of TML and moisture content: TML test ≤6 months old · moisture sample ≤7 days before loading (≤3 days if heavy rain)
  - Sampling and testing procedures by the shipper
- Sec 5 Trimming procedures (cohesive / non-cohesive cargo, angle of repose ≤30° shifting rule)
- Sec 6 Methods of determining angle of repose
- Sec 7 Cargoes that may liquefy (Group A):
  - Moisture content must be < TML · specially constructed or fitted ships may carry above TML
  - Can test (master's simple check) is no substitute for lab tests
- Sec 8 Test procedures for cargoes that may liquefy (Appendix 2: flow table, penetration, Proctor/Fagerberg)
- Sec 9 Materials possessing chemical hazards (classes 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 5.1, 6.1, 7, 8, 9 and MHB) — segregation table 9.3.3
- Sec 10 Carriage of solid bulk wastes (Basel Convention documents)
- Sec 11 Security provisions
- Sec 12 Stowage factor conversion tables
- Sec 13 References to related information and recommendations

**Appendices**
- Appendix 1 Individual Schedules (alphabetical by BCSN): description, characteristics (group, class, angle of repose, bulk density), hazard, stowage & segregation, hold cleanliness, weather precautions, loading, precautions, ventilation, carriage, discharge, clean-up, emergency procedures
- Appendix 2 Laboratory test procedures · Appendix 3 Properties of solid bulk cargoes · Appendix 4 Index · Appendix 5 BCSN in other languages
- Supplement: BLU Code & Manual · Recommendations on fumigation · Emergency schedules (EmS) · MFAG

**Amendment 08-25 (mandatory 1 Jan 2027)**
- 12 new schedules: incl. Apatite Concentrate, Asphalt Granulates, Fish Meal (Stabilized), Iron Ore Briquettes, Phosphate Rock Fines (uncalcined), Zinc Slag (Coarse)
- 11 schedules amended: incl. Ferrosilicon, Iron Ore Pellets, Castor Beans/Meal, DRI (A) and (B)
- Sec 3 fumigation guidance updated · Sec 9 segregation table 9.3.3 revised (radioactive separation clearer, "MHB" references removed)

**Cargoes not listed**
- A cargo with no schedule needs a tripartite agreement: competent authorities of the loading port, the flag State and the unloading port (provisional assessment)

**Key Point**
- Never load Group A cargo without a valid TML + moisture certificate; reject if wet, if sample dates are old, or if can test shows free water
- Liquefaction = sudden list and capsize (nickel ore, bauxite, iron ore fines casualties)
- Coal: monitor temperature, CH₄, O₂, CO; surface-ventilate only per schedule
- Carry the latest amendment on board (08-25 voluntary 2026, mandatory 2027)
