**ISPS Code — International Ship and Port Facility Security Code**

**What is the ISPS Code**
- International framework for ships and port facilities to detect security threats and take preventive measures
- Mandatory under SOLAS Ch XI-2 · Part A mandatory · Part B guidance (some flags/US/EU make parts of B mandatory)
- Ship: Ship Security Assessment (SSA) → Ship Security Plan (SSP) → International Ship Security Certificate (ISSC)
- Port: Port Facility Security Assessment (PFSA) → Port Facility Security Plan (PFSP) → Statement of Compliance of a Port Facility

**History**
- Response to 11 Sep 2001 attacks
- Adopted 12 Dec 2002 (SOLAS Diplomatic Conference, with SOLAS XI-1 and XI-2 amendments)
- In force 1 Jul 2004
- Related: SOLAS XI-1/3 IMO ship number, XI-1/5 Continuous Synopsis Record, V/19 AIS, XI-2/6 Ship Security Alert System (SSAS)
- Cyber risk: addressed via ISM (MSC.428(98)) and IMO guidelines MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3/Rev.3

**Applies to**
- Passenger ships (incl. HSC) on international voyages
- Cargo ships (incl. HSC) ≥500 GT on international voyages
- Mobile offshore drilling units
- Port facilities serving these ships
- Not covered: warships, naval auxiliaries, State ships on non-commercial service

**Security levels**
- Level 1: normal — minimum protective measures at all times
- Level 2: heightened risk — additional measures for a period
- Level 3: probable or imminent incident — specific measures, limited period (follow government instructions)
- Ship's level must be at least the port's level

---

**Part A — Mandatory**
- 1 General · 2 Definitions (SSP, SSO, CSO, PFSO, Declaration of Security)
- 3 Application
- 4 Responsibilities of Contracting Governments (set security levels, approve SSPs, issue ISSC)
- 5 Declaration of Security (DoS): agreement ship–port or ship–ship on security measures (when levels differ, ship–ship activity, threat, government request) · kept ≥ period set by flag
- 6 Obligations of the Company: master's overriding authority on security · support CSO, SSO and master
- 7 Ship security: measures at each level for access control, restricted areas, handling of cargo and stores, monitoring, embarkation of persons and baggage
- 8 Ship Security Assessment (by CSO): on-scene survey, identify threats, weaknesses, existing measures
- 9 Ship Security Plan: approved by flag/RSO · protected from unauthorized disclosure · contents include:
  - Measures to prevent weapons and dangerous substances on board · prevent unauthorized access
  - Procedures for responding to threats, evacuation, reporting incidents
  - Duties of personnel, audits, training, drills
  - SSAS activation points and procedures (confidential)
- 10 Records (kept ≥ period set by flag; in working language): training and drills · security threats and incidents · breaches · changes in security level · communications · internal audits · reviews of SSA/SSP · maintenance of security equipment · DoS
- 11 Company Security Officer (CSO)
- 12 Ship Security Officer (SSO): regular inspections, maintains SSP, coordinates with PFSO, reports incidents
- 13 Training, drills and exercises:
  - Drills at least every 3 months · when >25% crew changed with no prior drill on that ship, drill within 1 week
  - Exercises at least once each calendar year, ≤18 months between
- 14 Port facility security · 15 PFSA · 16 PFSP · 17 Port Facility Security Officer (PFSO) · 18 Port facility training and drills
- 19 Verification and certification for ships:
  - Initial, renewal (≤5 years), intermediate (between 2nd and 3rd anniversary) verifications
  - ISSC valid ≤5 years · Interim ISSC ≤6 months (no extension)

**Part B — Guidance**
- Detailed guidance on SSA, SSP contents, DoS, PFSA, information to be provided for control measures
- B/4: CSR and pre-arrival security information
- Many PSC regimes check Part B items (e.g., US 33 CFR 104)

**Linked SOLAS XI-2 regulations**
- XI-2/5 Specific responsibility of companies (master has information on who appoints crew, charterers)
- XI-2/6 Ship Security Alert System (covert, to flag/company; no alarm on board)
- XI-2/8 Master's discretion for ship safety and security
- XI-2/9 Control and compliance measures (pre-arrival information, delay, detention, denial of entry)

**Key Point**
- Know the port's security level and match it; DoS when levels differ
- Gangway watch, visitor log, ID checks, restricted areas locked
- Drill every 3 months; 25% new crew rule; one exercise a year
- SSP is confidential — PSC may only see it with flag consent (except listed sections)
- SSAS tested as per flag instructions; activation points known only to master/SSO and few others
