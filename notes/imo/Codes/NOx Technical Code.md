**NOx Technical Code 2008**

**Status**
- Checked against the IMO resolution text (MEPC.177(58)): dates, chapters, appendices, power threshold
- Tier limits come from MARPOL Annex VI Regulation 13, written from knowledge; to be verified

**What is the NOx Technical Code**
- Rules for testing, surveying and certifying marine diesel engines for nitrogen oxide emissions
- Mandatory under MARPOL Annex VI Regulation 13

**History**
- 1997 Code with Annex VI
- Revised 2008 Code adopted 10 Oct 2008, resolution MEPC.177(58)
- In force 1 Jul 2010

**Applies to**
- Marine diesel engines with power output of more than 130 kW, other than engines used only for emergencies

**Contents**
- Introduction; abbreviations, subscripts and symbols
- Chapter 1 General (purpose, application, definitions)
- Chapter 2 Surveys and certification
- Chapter 3 NOx emission standards (limits and test cycles)
- Chapter 4 Approval for serially manufactured engines (engine family and engine group concepts)
- Chapter 5 Procedures for NOx emission measurements on a test bed
- Chapter 6 Procedures for demonstrating compliance with NOx limits on board
- Chapter 7 Certification of an existing engine
- Appendices I to VIII
  - I EIAPP Certificate form
  - II survey and certification flowcharts
  - III analyser specifications
  - IV calibration of instruments
  - V parent engine test report and data
  - VI exhaust gas mass flow calculation (carbon-balance method)
  - VII checklist for the engine parameter check method
  - VIII direct measurement and monitoring method

**Tiers (MARPOL Annex VI Regulation 13)**
- Tier I: ships built 1 Jan 2000 to 31 Dec 2010
- Tier II: ships built from 1 Jan 2011
- Tier III: ships built from 1 Jan 2016, when operating in NOx Emission Control Areas (North American and US Caribbean Sea ECAs; Baltic and North Sea ECAs for ships built from 1 Jan 2021)

**Documents**
- EIAPP Certificate (Engine International Air Pollution Prevention)
- Technical File and record book of engine parameters

**Key Point**
- The engine on board must match its Technical File; changes need approval
