## NOx limits (Annex VI, regulation 13)
Limits in g/kWh by rated engine speed n (rpm).

![[diagram:nox-tiers|NOx limits at 720 rpm]]

| Tier | Ship constructed | n below 130 | 130 ≤ n below 2,000 | n 2,000 or more | At 720 rpm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | On or after 1 Jan 2000 | 17.0 | 45 · n^(−0.2) | 9.8 | 12.1 |
| II | On or after 1 Jan 2011 | 14.4 | 44 · n^(−0.23) | 7.7 | 9.7 |
| III | On or after 1 Jan 2016 (in NOx ECAs) | 3.4 | 9 · n^(−0.2) | 2.0 | 2.4 |

- NOx controls apply to installed marine diesel engines over 130 kW, other than those used solely for emergency purposes, regardless of ship tonnage

### Where Tier III applies (NOx Emission Control Areas)
| ECA | Tier III applies to ships constructed |
|---|---|
| North American ECA and US Caribbean Sea ECA | On or after 1 Jan 2016 |
| Baltic Sea ECA and North Sea ECA | On or after 1 Jan 2021 |
| Canadian Arctic ECA (amendments effective 1 Mar 2026) | Keel laid on or after 1 Jan 2025 (per DNV) |
| Norwegian Sea ECA (amendments effective 1 Mar 2026) | Contract on or after 1 Mar 2026, or, with no contract, keel laid on or after 1 Sep 2026, or delivered on or after 1 Mar 2030 (per DNV) |

- Outside these areas, Tier II applies
- The North-East Atlantic ECA was still pending at the time of checking (adjourned MEPC session 16–27 Nov 2026)

### MEPC.398(83) in detail
- **7.1** (existing renumbered 7.1.1–7.1.6): existing engines certified under regulation 13.7
- **7.2** (new 7.2.1–7.2.12): engines that undergo **substantial modification**, or are certified to a Tier they did not hold at installation
  - Applies to individual engines or engine group parent engines, not engine families
  - Nameplate replaced if rated power or speed changes
  - The 100% load point may be set no lower than 85% of rated power. If it cannot be reached, testing is deferred.
  - The engine emission test plan is agreed with the Administration before testing
- Parties were invited to consider early application

## Chapters 4–7
- **4 Serially manufactured engines:** engine family (4.3) and engine group (4.4) concepts, with a parent engine
- **5 Test-bed measurements**
- **6 On-board demonstration of compliance**
  - 6.3 Simplified measurement is limited to on-board confirmation tests and to renewal, annual and intermediate surveys (6.3.1.1). The allowance is 10% of the limit value, with a combined allowance with RM-grade fuel of no more than 15% (6.3.11).
  - 6.4 Direct measurement and monitoring: NOx is the minimum species measured, CO₂ added if the carbon balance method is used (6.4.2) · load points within ±5% of rated power, or +0/−10% at 100% load (6.4.6.7) · data averaged over a stable 10-minute interval per load point (6.4.9.2) · data current within 30 days and kept on board at least 3 months (6.4.16.1)
- **7 Certification of existing engines (regulation 13.7)** and, since 1 Sep 2026, substantial modification (7.2): application to the Administration (7.1) · the Approved Method File travels with the engine and includes an on-board verification description · a post-installation survey leads to amending the IAPP Certificate
