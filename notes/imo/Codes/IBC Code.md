**IBC Code — International Code for the Construction and Equipment of Ships Carrying Dangerous Chemicals in Bulk**

**What is the IBC Code**
- IMO code for design, construction, equipment and operation of chemical tankers carrying dangerous chemicals and noxious liquid substances (NLS) in bulk
- Mandatory under SOLAS Ch VII Part B (safety) and MARPOL Annex II (pollution)
- Certificate: International Certificate of Fitness for the Carriage of Dangerous Chemicals in Bulk (CoF) — lists the products the ship may carry

**History**
- BCH Code (1971) for ships built before 1 Jul 1986
- IBC Code adopted 1983 (MSC.4(48), MEPC.19(22)) · applies to ships built on/after 1 Jul 1986
- Major revision 2007 (MSC.219(82)/MEPC.166(56)) with revised MARPOL Annex II — new pollution categories X, Y, Z, OS · in force 1 Jan 2007
- Amendments incl. MSC.460(101)/MEPC.318(74): revised Ch 17/18 product listings, in force 1 Jan 2021
- Product list updated each year via MEPC.2/Circ. (tripartite agreements, provisional assessments)

**Applies to**
- Ships of any size carrying in bulk products listed in Ch 17 (safety and/or pollution hazards)
- Ships built on/after 1 Jul 1986 (older ships: BCH Code)
- Not for: petroleum or similar flammable products (MARPOL Annex I), liquefied gases (IGC)

---

**Chapters**
- Ch 1 General: definitions, application, surveys and certification (CoF valid ≤5 years; annual and intermediate surveys)
- Ch 2 Ship survival capability and location of cargo tanks:
  - Ship types by hazard: **Type 1** most severe · **Type 2** appreciably severe · **Type 3** sufficiently severe
  - Tank location: Type 1 — ≥B/5 or 11.5 m (less) from side shell and ≥B/15 or 6 m (less) from bottom, never <760 mm · Type 2 — ≥760 mm from side, ≥B/15 or 6 m from bottom · Type 3 — no requirement
  - Damage stability assumptions per ship type · freeboard and intact stability
- Ch 3 Ship arrangements: cargo segregation (cofferdams), accommodation and machinery away from cargo, cargo pump rooms, access to spaces
- Ch 4 Cargo containment: tank types (independent, integral, gravity, pressure)
- Ch 5 Cargo transfer: piping scantlings, fabrication, flange connections, shut-off valves, hoses, bow/stern loading
- Ch 6 Materials of construction, protective linings and coatings (compatibility per product)
- Ch 7 Cargo temperature control (heating/cooling)
- Ch 8 Cargo tank venting and gas-freeing: open venting · controlled venting (P/V valves, high-velocity vents) · flame screens
- Ch 9 Environmental control: inerting, padding, drying, ventilation
- Ch 10 Electrical installations (hazardous areas, temperature class, apparatus group)
- Ch 11 Fire protection and fire extinction (deck foam system, cargo pump room fixed system)
- Ch 12 Mechanical ventilation in the cargo area (30 air changes/hour for cargo pump rooms)
- Ch 13 Instrumentation: gauging (open, restricted, closed), vapour detection, overflow control (high-level alarms at 95%)
- Ch 14 Personnel protection: protective equipment, safety equipment (breathing apparatus, escape sets), emergency eye wash and showers
- Ch 15 Special requirements for specific products (e.g., ammonium nitrate solution, carbon disulphide, hydrogen peroxide, sulphur (molten), phosphorus)
- Ch 16 Operational requirements: max allowable tank filling, cargo information (product data, Safety Data Sheet), personnel training, opening tanks
- Ch 17 Summary of minimum requirements — product table: product name · pollution category (X/Y/Z) · hazards (S/P) · ship type · tank type · venting · environmental control · electrical · gauging · vapour detection · fire protection · special requirements
- Ch 18 List of products to which the Code does not apply (pollution only — MARPOL Annex II)
- Ch 19 Index of products
- Ch 20 Transport of liquid chemical wastes
- Ch 21 Criteria for assigning carriage requirements (GESAMP hazard profiles)

**MARPOL Annex II link**
- Pollution categories: X (major hazard — prohibited discharge), Y (limited discharge), Z (minor), OS (other substances)
- P&A Manual (Procedures and Arrangements) · Cargo Record Book · prewash for Category X (and solidifying/high-viscosity Y)
- Stripping: residue ≤75 L for new ships (built ≥1 Jan 2007)

**Key Point**
- Before loading: product must be on the CoF list · check Ch 17 row (ship type, tank coating compatibility, inerting, vapour detection)
- Cargo info and SDS on board · crew trained for the product's hazards (toxic, corrosive, reactive)
- Tank cleaning and prewash per P&A Manual, logged in the Cargo Record Book (surveyor endorsement for prewash)
