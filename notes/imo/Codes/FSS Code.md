**FSS Code — International Code for Fire Safety Systems**

**What is the FSS Code**
- Engineering specifications for fire safety systems required by SOLAS Ch II-2
- Mandatory under SOLAS II-2/3.22 (from 1 Jul 2002)
- SOLAS II-2 says which systems a ship needs; FSS says how each system must be designed and perform

**History**
- Adopted 5 Dec 2000 (MSC.98(73)) · in force 1 Jul 2002 (with revised SOLAS II-2)
- Ch 15 Inert gas systems revised (MSC.367(93)) · Ch 17 helideck foam (MSC.403(96))
- MSC.555(108) with SOLAS II-2 MSC.550(108): in force 1 Jan 2026 — ro-ro and vehicle spaces: improved fire detection (individually identifiable detectors), permanent video monitoring, protected openings, water monitors on weather decks (ships keel laid ≥1 Jan 2026)

**Applies to**
- Fire safety systems on SOLAS ships per SOLAS II-2 (new ships: in force date of each amendment)

---

**Chapters**
- Ch 1 General · definitions
- Ch 2 International shore connection: flange OD 178 mm, ID 64 mm, bolt circle 132 mm, 4 holes 19 mm · pressure 1.0 N/mm² · with gasket, 4 bolts 16 mm × 50 mm, 8 washers · kept on board ready to use on either side
- Ch 3 Personnel protection:
  - Fire-fighter's outfit: protective clothing, boots, helmet, electric safety lamp (≥3 h), axe
  - Breathing apparatus: SCBA ≥1,200 L free air (≥30 min) · spare charges
  - Emergency escape breathing devices (EEBD): ≥10 min, hood or face mask
- Ch 4 Fire extinguishers: portable — capacity ≤13.5 kg (powder/CO₂), fire rating standards · spare charges
- Ch 5 Fixed gas fire-extinguishing systems:
  - CO₂: machinery spaces — 85% of gas discharged within 2 min · quantity 40% (gross volume) / 35% (incl. casing) · cargo spaces 30% gross volume, 2/3 within 10 min
  - Two separate controls; release alarm and time delay (≥20 s) before release into normally manned spaces
- Ch 6 Fixed foam fire-extinguishing systems: high-expansion (machinery), low-expansion (deck)
- Ch 7 Fixed pressure water-spraying and water-mist systems (machinery spaces, cargo pump rooms, special category spaces)
- Ch 8 Automatic sprinkler, fire detection and fire alarm systems (accommodation, service spaces, passenger ships)
- Ch 9 Fixed fire detection and fire alarm systems:
  - Detectors: heat (54–78 °C trigger), smoke, flame · individually identifiable detectors (ships built ≥1 Jul 2010 passenger cabins; extended)
  - Two power supplies (main + emergency) · audible and visual alarms at the control station · fault signals
- Ch 10 Sample extraction smoke detection systems (cargo holds)
- Ch 11 Low-location lighting (passenger ships, escape routes)
- Ch 12 Fixed emergency fire pumps: capacity ≥40% of main fire pumps (min 25 m³/h) · two hydrants with required pressure · own fuel supply ≥3 h (cargo ships)
- Ch 13 Arrangement of means of escape (passenger ships — evacuation analysis)
- Ch 14 Fixed deck foam systems (tankers): foam supply ≥20 min · monitors and applicators
- Ch 15 Inert gas systems (tankers): O₂ ≤5% in supply · tanks kept ≤8% O₂ and positive pressure · deck water seal, non-return valves, P/V breakers
- Ch 16 Fixed hydrocarbon gas detection systems (double-hull spaces of oil tankers)
- Ch 17 Helicopter facility foam fire-fighting appliances

**Linked: SOLAS II-2**
- Reg 7 Detection and alarm · Reg 10 Fire fighting (fire mains, pumps, hydrants, hoses) · Reg 15 Instructions, training, drills · Reg 20 Vehicle, special category and ro-ro spaces (2026 changes)
- Maintenance: MSC.1/Circ.1432 (revised guidelines for maintenance and inspection of fire protection systems) — weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual checks

**Key Point**
- International shore connection on board and ready
- CO₂ release only after headcount and closing the space; alarm and delay must work
- EEBDs and SCBA charged and checked; fire-fighter outfits complete (lamp, axe)
- New ro-ro/vehicle carriers (keel ≥1 Jan 2026): video monitoring and identifiable detectors in vehicle spaces
