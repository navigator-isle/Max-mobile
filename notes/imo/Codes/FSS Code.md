# FSS Code — International Code for Fire Safety Systems

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Adopted | 5 Dec 2000, resolution MSC.98(73) |
| In force | 1 Jul 2002, with the revised SOLAS chapter II-2 |
| Status | Mandatory under revised SOLAS chapter II-2 |
| Structure | 17 chapters (1–15 original; 16 added 2012, 17 added 2020) |
| Latest amendment | MSC.555(108), in force 1 Jan 2026 (chapters 7 and 9) |
| Role | SOLAS II-2 says which systems a ship needs; the FSS Code says how they are built and perform |

## What is the FSS Code

- Gives **international standards for the fire safety systems** required by SOLAS chapter II-2
- The technical system requirements are in the Code, while the statutory requirements stay in the Convention
- Some original technical provisions were moved from the Convention into the Code
- Chapter 1 also covers general provisions: applicability, definitions, equivalents and alternative designs, and a ban on toxic extinguishing media

- Kept on board with a gasket, four bolts, four nuts and eight washers.

## Chapter 3 — Personnel protection

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Fire-fighter's outfit | Protective clothing with a water-resistant outer surface; rubber or non-conducting boots; rigid impact-resistant helmet; electric safety lamp with at least **3 h** burning time (explosion-proof on tankers and hazardous areas); insulated axe |
| Breathing apparatus | Compressed-air SCBA with at least **1,200 litres** of air in the cylinders, or another SCBA that runs at least **30 min**; cylinders interchangeable |
| Lifeline | Fireproof, at least 30 m long, proof-tested at 3.5 kN for 5 min, with a snap-hook |
| EEBD | At least **10 min** service duration; flame-resistant hood or full face piece with a clear viewing window; for **escape only**, not for fire-fighting or entering oxygen-deficient spaces |

## Chapter 4 — Fire extinguishers

- Powder or CO₂ extinguisher: at least 5 kg. Foam extinguisher: at least 9 litres.
- Portable extinguishers must not exceed **23 kg** and must be at least as effective as a 9-litre fluid extinguisher.
- Portable foam applicator: inductor-type nozzle on the fire main, a tank of at least 20 litres of foam liquid plus one spare tank, output at least 1.5 m³/min.

## Chapter 5 — Fixed gas systems (CO₂)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cargo spaces | Free gas volume at least **30%** of the largest protected cargo space |
| Machinery spaces | The larger of **40%** of the largest machinery space (casing excluded above the 40% horizontal-area level) or **35%** including the casing. Cargo ships under 2,000 GT with two or more non-separate machinery spaces: 35% and 30% |
| Calculation | Free CO₂ volume at 0.56 m³/kg |
| Discharge | Machinery spaces: **85%** of the gas within **2 min** |
| Controls | Two separate controls in a release box: one opens the space valve, the other releases the gas. Key in a break-glass enclosure |
| Alarm | Audible pre-discharge alarm in ro-ro and occupied spaces, at least **20 s** before release |
| Automatic release | Not permitted unless the Administration allows it |
| Pipes | Through accommodation: welded, substantial thickness, pressure-tested at not less than 5 N/mm² |

## Chapters 6 to 8 — Foam, water-spray, sprinklers

| Chapter | Key figures |
|---|---|
| 6 Fixed foam | **High-expansion:** fill the largest space at least 1 m depth per minute; foam volume five times the largest space; expansion ratio not above 1,000:1. **Low-expansion:** 150 mm depth over the largest oil-spread area within 5 min; ratio not above 12:1 |
| 7 Water-spray and water-mist | Average at least 5 l/m²/min in machinery spaces; pump starts automatically on pressure drop; pump and controls outside the protected spaces |
| 8 Sprinklers, detection and alarm | Wet-pipe systems (saunas dry-pipe, heads up to 140 °C); spare heads 6 (fewer than 300 installed), 12 (300–1,000), 24 (over 1,000); operating range 68–79 °C in accommodation; sections of at most 200 sprinklers, and on passenger ships at most two decks; two power sources |

## Chapters 9 and 10 — Detection

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Heat detectors | Operate before **78 °C**, not below **54 °C**, at a rise under 1 °C/min; drying rooms up to 130 °C, saunas up to 140 °C |
| Smoke detectors | In stairways, corridors and escape routes: operate before 12.5%/m obscuration, not below 2%/m |
| Spacing | Heat: 37 m² each, 9 m apart, 4.5 m from bulkheads. Smoke: 74 m², 11 m, 5.5 m |
| Power | Two sources, one of them emergency |
| Alarm | An unacknowledged alarm sounds throughout accommodation, service, control and category A machinery spaces after **2 min** |
| Panel | On the bridge or in a manned central control station |
| Sample extraction (Ch 10) | Sensing unit operates before 6.65%/m obscuration; duplicate fans and alternative power; sampling pipes at least 12 mm internal diameter |

## Chapters 11 to 13

- **Ch 11 Low-location lighting:** approved under IMO guidelines (A.752(18)) or ISO 15370:2001.
- **Ch 12 Emergency fire pumps:** capacity at least **40%** of the required fire pump capacity, and not less than 25 m³/h (passenger ships under 1,000 GT and cargo ships of 2,000 GT and above) or 15 m³/h (cargo ships under 2,000 GT). Diesel drive must start by hand at 0 °C, at least six times within 30 min, including twice in the first 10 min. Service tank holds 3 h at full load, with reserve fuel outside the machinery space for 15 h more.
- **Ch 13 Means of escape:** passenger ships' stairways at least 900 mm wide, plus 10 mm per person above 90; at most 3.5 m rise without a landing; inclination at most 45°; handrails on both sides. Cargo ships: stairways and corridors at least 700 mm wide, handrail on one side (both sides at 1,800 mm and over).

## Chapters 14 to 17 — Tankers and helicopters

| Chapter | Key figures |
|---|---|
| 14 Fixed deck foam | Solution rate: the greatest of 0.6 l/min/m² of cargo tank deck area, 6 l/min/m² of the largest tank's sectional area, or 3 l/min/m² of the monitor-protected area (at least 1,250 l/min). Foam supply **20 min** with inert gas, **30 min** without. Expansion ratio generally not above 12:1. Applicators at least 400 l/min each, throw at least 15 m, at least four |
| 15 Inert gas | Cargo tank atmosphere at **8% oxygen or less** by volume and positive pressure. Supply at 125% of maximum discharge rate, with no more than **5%** oxygen in the supply main. At least two blowers and two non-return devices, one a water seal. Alarms for oxygen above 8%, gas pressure below 100 mm water gauge, and high pressure; automatic shutdown of blowers and the gas regulating valve |
| 16 Fixed hydrocarbon gas detection | Central gas measurement unit with sampling pipes in ballast tanks and void spaces of double-hull and double-bottom spaces next to cargo tanks, including the forepeak. At least two sampling points per space (upper at least 1 m from tank top, lower at least 0.5 m from tank bottom). Each line analysed in turn at intervals not over 30 min. Alarm at a pre-set value not over **30% of the LFL**; separate alarm for clogged lines |
| 17 Helicopter facility foam | Foam systems protecting helidecks and landing areas: discharge rates, foam concentrate quantities, nozzle types, equipment height limits |

## The 2026 amendments (MSC.555(108), with SOLAS MSC.550(108))

Both resolutions take effect 1 Jan 2026. MSC.555(108) updates FSS chapters 7 and 9.

| Area | New requirement |
|---|---|
| Ch 7, new 2.5 | Fixed water-based systems on weather decks carrying vehicles on ro-ro passenger ships built on or after 1 Jan 2026: monitors at least 2.0 l/min per m² of protected area, none below 1,250 l/min; monitors outside the protected area; far end within 75% of the monitor's still-air throw; areas monitors cannot reach need nozzles giving 5.0 l/min per m²; four jets where dangerous goods are carried on the weather deck |
| Ch 9, 2.3.1.3 to 2.3.1.5 | Heat detector tests to EN 54:2001 and IEC 60092-504; **new** linear heat detectors tested to EN 54-22:2015 and IEC 60092-504 |
| Ch 9, 2.4.2.2 | Revised spacing: heat 37 m², 9 m, 4.5 m · smoke 74 m², 11 m, 5.5 m · combined smoke and heat 74 m², 9 m, 4.5 m · linear heat cable not more than 9.0 m apart and 4.5 m from bulkheads |
| Ch 9, 2.5.1.2 to 2.5.1.4 | Ro-ro passenger ships built from 1 Jan 2026: alarms shown consistently and recognisable on the bridge; alarm addressability, history, latest alarm and suppression; smoke detection in special category and ro-ro spaces may be disconnected during loading and unloading (time-limited, resets automatically, shown on the central unit); heat detection and manual call points cannot be disconnected |

**SOLAS II-2 side (UK MGN 713):** passenger ships built from 1 Jan 2026 need individually addressable smoke and heat detection in vehicle, special category and ro-ro spaces, and continuous video monitoring with footage replayable for at least 7 days. Older passenger ships need water monitors by their first survey from 1 Jan 2028 and video monitoring by 1 Jan 2028 with 24 hours of replay.

> **Key Point:** Keep the international shore connection on board and ready. EEBDs are for escape only. CO₂ is released only from two separate controls, after the pre-discharge alarm has run at least 20 seconds. Inert gas: at most 8% oxygen in the tanks and at most 5% in the supply main.
