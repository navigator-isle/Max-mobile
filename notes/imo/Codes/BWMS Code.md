# BWMS Code — Code for Approval of Ballast Water Management Systems

## At a glance
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Adopted | **13 Apr 2018**, MEPC 72, resolution **MEPC.300(72)** (Annex 5 of MEPC 72/17/Add.1) |
| Takes effect | **13 Oct 2019**, on entry into force of the associated amendments |
| Replaces | The **2016 Guidelines (G8)**, MEPC.279(70), revoked when the Code takes effect. References to G8 in other IMO instruments now mean the BWMS Code. |
| Status | Mandatory through the [[BWM 2004]] Convention, regulation D-3 |
| Purpose | Type approval of ballast water management systems so they meet the **D-2 standard** |
| Result | **Type Approval Certificate** with the system's design limitations |
| Structure | 8 sections, Annex Parts 1–7, and the Type Approval Certificate appendix |

![[diagram:bwms-approval|From test to certificate]]

> **Key Point:** The Code tells Administrations how to test and approve a ballast water treatment system: pre-test documents, **land-based tests**, **shipboard tests**, environmental tests, self-monitoring and validation of **System Design Limitations (SDL)**. Systems using Active Substances also need IMO approval first.

## The D-2 standard behind the tests (Section 2)
![[diagram:bwms-d2|D-2 discharge limits]]

| Organisms or microbe | Limit |
|---|---|
| Viable organisms **50 µm or more** | Fewer than **10 per m³** |
| Viable organisms **10 to 50 µm** | Fewer than **10 per mL** |
| Toxicogenic *Vibrio cholerae* (O1, O139) | Fewer than **1 cfu per 100 mL** (or per 1 g zooplankton wet weight) |
| *Escherichia coli* | Fewer than **250 cfu per 100 mL** |
| Intestinal enterococci | Fewer than **100 cfu per 100 mL** |

## Transitional provisions
- Systems approved by **28 Oct 2018** under the old G8 (MEPC.174(58)) may be **installed before 28 Oct 2020**
- "Installed" means the **contractual delivery date** to the ship, or the actual delivery date if there is no contract date
- Systems approved under the **2016 G8 are deemed to comply** with the Code (1.13). Makers of such systems submit only the additional tests and documents (6.12).
- A type approval certificate expiring does **not** affect the operational validity of systems already installed. They need not be renewed or replaced for that reason (preamble).

## Sections
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 Introduction | Aim is regulation D-3 and the D-2 standard. Applies to all ships under D-2. Approval screens out systems that would fail D-2 but does not guarantee performance on every ship (1.5). |
| 2 Background | The D-2 limits |
| 3 Definitions | BWMS, Active Substance, failed, valid and invalid test cycle, **System Design Limitations**, **Treatment Rated Capacity (TRC)** |
| 4 Technical specifications | Effective on short and long voyages whatever the temperature. Safe design, alarms, maintenance access, seals and bypass alarms, calibration. Automatic recording, 24-month data storage, gas detection per IEC 60079-29-1. |
| 5 Type approval process | Pre-test evaluation, land-based and shipboard tests, SDL on the certificate, installation survey |
| 6 Approval and certification | Type Approval Certificate. Active Substance systems need prior IMO approval under D-3.2. Reports go to IMO. |
| 7 Installation requirements | Sampling facilities. Any **bypass must raise an alarm and be logged**, except internal transfers such as anti-heeling. |
| 8 Installation survey and commissioning | Documents kept on board and checks before the International Ballast Water Management Certificate is issued |

## Annex parts
| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-test documentation. Major components cannot change during testing. A change means new evaluation and repeated tests (1.10). |
| 2 | Test and performance specifications |
| 3 | Environmental testing of electrical and electronic parts at an accredited laboratory. Reference standards IACS UR E10 and ISO/IEC 17025. |
| 4 | Sample analysis. A cycle succeeds only if **all D-2 limits are met**, with no averaging or discounting of failed cycles (4.7). |
| 5 | Self-monitoring. Data tamper-resistant, time-stamped and kept at least **24 months** (5.7–5.8). |
| 6 | SDL validation, overseen by the Administration, independent of the maker, not a stress test |
| 7 | Format of the Type Approval Certificate and the type approval report |

## Test requirements (Part 2)
![[diagram:bwms-tests|Key figures in the test programme]]

### Land-based tests
- **At least 5 consecutive successful cycles in each salinity** (2.11)
- At least **2 cycles per salinity** at the minimum holding time (2.13)
- Control and treated tanks each hold at least **200 m³** (2.21)
- No downscaling for models of 200 m³/h or less (2.26)
- Test facility independent of the manufacturer (2.4)
- Samples analysed live within **6 hours** (2.44)

| Test water | Salinity |
|---|---|
| Marine | **28–36 PSU** |
| Brackish | **10–20 PSU** |
| Fresh | **below 1 PSU** |

- An **unlimited** certificate needs tests in all three salinity ranges, separated by at least 10 PSU (2.31)
- **Dissolved and particulate organic carbon:** above 1 mg/L (marine) or 5 mg/L (brackish and fresh). **Total suspended solids:** above 1 mg/L (marine) or 50 mg/L (brackish and fresh).
- **Challenge organisms:**
  - 50 µm and above: preferably 10⁶, not less than 10⁵ per m³, from at least **5 species in 3 phyla**
  - 10–50 µm: preferably 10⁴, not less than 10³ per mL
  - heterotrophic bacteria: at least 10⁴ per mL
  - coliform, *Enterococcus* and *V. cholerae* are measured, not added
- **Temperature:** effective operation from **0–40 °C** (2–40 °C for fresh water)
- **Regrowth:** assessed in at least 2 cycles per salinity, with holding time of at least **5 days** or enumeration at least 5 days after treatment (2.49–2.52)
- A control cycle discharging at or below **10 times the D-2.1 limit** makes the cycle invalid (2.45)

### Shipboard tests
- Test cycles span at least **6 months** (2.8.7)
- **3 consecutive compliant cycles** required (2.8.8)
- Uptake water must exceed **10 times** the D-2.1 limits for the test to be valid (2.8.5)
- Sample volumes: at least **1 m³ influent and 3 m³ discharge** for 50 µm and above, and at least **10 L** for 10–50 µm (2.8.6)
- The shipboard test unit should be at the upper limit of TRC unless the Administration approves otherwise (4.14–4.16)

## Type Approval Certificate (Part 7.1)
- Identifies the BWMS type and model, with dated assembly drawings
- References the full performance test protocol
- States installation conditions and limitations, and the SDL under the heading "This equipment has been designed for operation in the following conditions"
- Includes restrictions such as UV transmittance, pressure or TRO limits
- Appendix with results for each land-based and shipboard cycle
- Appendix form lists the **Treatment Rated Capacity in m³/h**, limiting operating conditions (temperature and salinity), signature, stamp, issue and validity dates, and a requirement to **keep a copy on board**
- The certificate cannot incorporate documents by reference (7.5). Proprietary information may be redacted (7.3).

## Approval of changes
- **Major components:** new test proposal and repeated land-based and shipboard tests (Part 1, 1.10)
- **Non-major components:** equivalent replacements allowed with reporting (1.11)
- **Safety upgrades:** allowed and reported. If they affect D-2 performance they count as major changes (1.12).
- **Software:** changes after pre-test evaluation follow a traceable change-handling procedure (4.22)

## Not included (not verified against the original text)
- Any amendment to the Code after 2018, such as later MEPC resolutions
- The "experience-building phase" and its dates, which did not appear in the Code text
- Whether a given system was installed under the Code or the older G8 on a particular ship. This depends on installation date and flag State practice.
- Active Substance approval under G9, which is a separate IMO procedure
- The "ten days holding time" often quoted. It is **not in the Code text**. The Code uses **5 days** for regrowth assessment.
