# ESP Code — 2011 International Code on the Enhanced Programme of Inspections during Surveys of Bulk Carriers and Oil Tankers

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Adopted | 30 Nov 2011, Assembly resolution A.1049(27) |
| In force | 1 Jan 2014 (takes effect with the SOLAS chapter XI-1 amendments) |
| Made mandatory by | SOLAS regulation XI-1/2 |
| Structure | Annex A (bulk carriers) · Annex B (oil tankers), each in Parts A and B |
| Consolidated text | Res. MSC.461(101), "completely revised", in force 1 Jan 2021 |
| Latest amendment | MSC.553(108), in force 1 Jan 2026 (thickness measurement firms) |
| Bulk carrier survey cycle | Renewal every 5 years · annual · intermediate |

## What is the ESP Code

- The enhanced programme of inspections during surveys of **bulk carriers and oil tankers**, mainly the hull structure
- Close-up surveys, thickness measurement, coating condition and corrosion assessment
- Surveys are planned and documented, and the documents stay on board
- The Maritime Safety Committee keeps the Code under review and updates it

## Structure

![[diagram:esp-structure|Annexes A and B, their Parts, and the eight sections in each part]]

| Annex | Part | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| A: Bulk carriers | A | Self-propelled ships of 500 GT and above, **other than** double-side skin bulk carriers |
| A: Bulk carriers | B | Double-side skin bulk carriers |
| B: Oil tankers | A | Double-hull oil tankers |
| B: Oil tankers | B | Oil tankers other than double-hull |

- Hybrid ships apply Part B to double-side skin holds and their wing spaces.
- Each part has eight sections: **1** General · **2** Renewal survey · **3** Annual survey · **4** Intermediate survey · **5** Preparations for survey · **6** Documentation on board · **7** Procedures for thickness measurements · **8** Reporting and evaluation of survey.
- Bulk carrier Part A has Annexes 1–15 and Part B has Annexes 1–12. Oil tanker Parts A and B each have Annexes 1–12, including tank testing and hull girder longitudinal strength criteria (Annex 12).

## Key definitions (Annex A, Part A)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Substantial corrosion | Wastage in excess of **75% of allowable margins**, but within acceptable limits. For ships under the IACS Common Structural Rules, gauged thickness between tnet + 0.5 mm and tnet |
| Close-up survey | Structural details within close visual range, normally within reach of hand |
| Overall survey | Reports the hull's overall condition and determines the extent of close-up surveys |
| Suspect areas | Locations showing substantial corrosion, or considered prone to rapid wastage |
| Coating condition | Rated GOOD, FAIR or POOR by breakdown and rust percentages (POOR, for example: general breakdown over 20% of an area, or hard scale at 10% or more) |
| Intermediate survey | Carried out at the second or third annual survey, or between them |

## Survey types (bulk carriers)

![[diagram:esp-cycle|Annual, intermediate and renewal surveys over the five-year cycle]]

| Survey | Rule |
|---|---|
| Renewal | 5-year cycle. May begin at the fourth annual survey and be completed by the fifth anniversary. At least two bottom inspections in the five years, with at most 36 months between them. For ships 15 years and older, the bottom inspection is done in dry-dock |
| Annual | Hull, weather decks, hatch covers, coamings and piping. Hatch cover sets in the forward 25% of the ship's length are surveyed open, closed and in operation each year |
| Intermediate, 5–10 years | Overall survey of representative ballast tanks, plus close-up examination of at least 25% of frames in the forward hold and one other selected hold |
| Intermediate, 10–15 years | Same extent as the previous renewal survey. Internal fuel tank examination and pressure testing not required unless the surveyor deems them necessary |
| Intermediate, over 15 years | As 10–15 years, plus a dry-dock survey as part of the intermediate survey |

## Planning, thickness measurement and documents

| Subject | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Survey programme (5.1) | Agreed in writing with the owner before the survey, based on Annex 4A; the owner completes the planning questionnaire (Annex 4B) first |
| Planning meeting (5.6) | Held before any part of a renewal or intermediate survey starts |
| Surveyors (1.4) | Bulk carriers of 20,000 dwt and above: two surveyors for the third and later renewal and intermediate surveys. 100,000 dwt and above: two surveyors for the 10–15-year intermediate survey |
| Thickness measurement | Normally ultrasonic. Done by a **certified company**, certification renewed at intervals not exceeding 3 years. Reports give locations, measured and original thicknesses, equipment and personnel and are signed by the operator; the surveyor countersigns the cover page |
| Firm approval (MSC.553(108)) | After reviewing a firm's documents, the Administration **must audit** the firm to confirm it is duly organized and managed and capable of hull thickness measurement. Applies to all annexes |
| Documents on board | The **survey report file** and supporting documents, kept on board for the ship's lifetime |
| Reporting | The Condition Evaluation Report must include an English translation and be endorsed by the Administration or recognized organization |

> **Key Point:** The ship keeps the survey report file and supporting documents on board for life. Prepare safe access and the owner's inspection records before surveys. Thickness measurement must be done by a certified firm. Substantial corrosion means wastage over 75% of the allowable margin.
