**IP Code (International Code of Safety for Industrial Personnel)**

**Status**
- Checked against the IMO resolution text (MSC.527(106)) and the MSC 111 outcome
- Corrected from the first draft: threshold, structure, and chapter list were wrong

**What is IP Code**
- Safety standards for industrial personnel carried on ships, such as offshore wind, oil and gas workers
- Mandatory under SOLAS Chapter XV
- Industrial personnel: all persons transported or accommodated on board for offshore industrial activities

**History**
- Adopted 10 Nov 2022 by MSC 106, resolution MSC.527(106)
- SOLAS Chapter XV adopted by MSC.521(106)
- In force 1 Jul 2024
- Amendment adopted at MSC 111 (May 2026): assumed mass of industrial personnel for stability is 90 kg per person for new ships from 1 Jan 2028

**Applies to**
- Ships of 500 GT and above carrying industrial personnel
- Ships under 500 GT carrying more than 12 industrial personnel (with passengers and special personnel): goals and functional requirements apply as far as practicable
- It adds to the SOLAS cargo ship rules; it covers the risks of transferring personnel that SOLAS does not

**Structure**
- Preamble
- Part I General: goal, definitions, certificate and surveys
- Part II Goals and functional requirements (9 chapters)
  - industrial personnel
  - safe transfer
  - subdivision and stability
  - machinery installations
  - electrical installations
  - periodically unattended machinery spaces
  - fire safety
  - life-saving appliances
  - dangerous goods
- Part III Regulations
  - Reg 1 Industrial personnel
  - Reg 2 Safe transfer
- Part IV Additional regulations for ships certified under SOLAS Chapter I (Regs 1 to 8)
  - separate requirements for ships certified for 60 and for 240 persons
- Part V Additional regulations for craft certified under SOLAS Chapter X (Regs 1 to 8)
  - high-speed craft limited to 60 persons on board
- Appendix: Industrial Personnel Safety Certificate and Record of Equipment (Form IP)

**Key Point**
- Industrial personnel get a safety induction and are counted in life-saving appliance capacity
- Stability uses the assumed mass per person; 90 kg for new ships from 1 Jan 2028

**Sources**
- IMO resolution MSC.527(106); Britannia P&I summary of MSC 111
