**IP Code — International Code of Safety for Ships Carrying Industrial Personnel**

**What is the IP Code**
- Safety standards for ships that carry **industrial personnel** — persons transported or accommodated on board for offshore industrial activities (offshore wind farms, oil and gas, construction)
- Fills the gap between cargo ship rules (≤12 passengers) and passenger ship rules
- Mandatory under SOLAS Chapter XV, with special attention to **personnel transfer** to other ships and offshore facilities

**History**
- Developed after the growth of offshore wind service operation vessels (SOVs) and crew transfer vessels
- Adopted 10 Nov 2022 by MSC 106: Code MSC.527(106); SOLAS Ch XV MSC.521(106)
- In force 1 Jul 2024
- MSC 111 (May 2026): amendment — dimensioning weight of an industrial person (with PPE) **90 kg** (instead of 75 kg) · in force 1 Jan 2028

**Applies to**
- Cargo ships and high-speed cargo craft of **500 GT and above** on international voyages carrying more than 12 industrial personnel (counted together with passengers and special personnel)
- Ships under 500 GT: the Administration decides; the goals and functional requirements apply as far as reasonable
- Not: passenger ships certified for the persons carried

---

**Structure**
- **Part I General**: goal, definitions (industrial personnel, offshore industrial activities, personnel transfer), application, **Industrial Personnel Safety Certificate** and surveys
- **Part II Goals and functional requirements**: 1 Industrial personnel · 2 Safe transfer · 3 Subdivision and stability · 4 Machinery installations · 5 Electrical installations · 6 Periodically unattended machinery spaces · 7 Fire safety · 8 Life-saving appliances · 9 Dangerous goods
- **Part III Regulations**: Reg 1 Industrial personnel · Reg 2 Safe transfer
- **Part IV** Additional requirements for ships certified under SOLAS Ch I (stability, fire, LSA by number of persons — ships carrying up to 60, and over 60 up to 240 persons)
- **Part V** Additional requirements for high-speed craft (SOLAS Ch X)
- Appendix: Form of Industrial Personnel Safety Certificate and Record of Equipment

**Industrial personnel (Reg 1)** — means must be provided so they:
- Are medically fit
- Can communicate with the ship's crew (understand safety information, symbols, signs and alarms)
- Have appropriate safety training (personal survival, fire safety, first aid — or a qualification acceptable to the Administration)
- Receive **ship-specific safety familiarization** before leaving port or right after boarding: ship layout; location of lifejackets, muster and embarkation stations, escape routes, first aid; signs and alarms; action on alarm
- Receive **familiarization with the transfer arrangements** before being transferred
- Master must have documents confirming their training before they sail

**Safe transfer (Reg 2)**
- Transfer appliances (walk-to-work gangways, boat landings, baskets) kept clean, maintained, permanently marked with limits; rigging checked
- Risk assessment of each transfer; weather limits; communication between ship and facility; rescue of a person in the water

**Key Point**
- Industrial personnel are not passengers and not crew — count them for LSA and stability (90 kg each for new ships from 2028)
- No industrial personnel on board without training documents and ship familiarization
- Transfers only within the assessed weather limits, with recovery means ready
