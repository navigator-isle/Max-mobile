**BLU Code (Code of Practice for the Safe Loading and Unloading of Bulk Carriers)**

**Status**
- Draft written from knowledge; to be checked against the IMO text (A.862(20))

**What is BLU**
- Practice for safe loading and unloading of solid bulk cargoes, between ship and terminal
- Mandatory under SOLAS Chapter VI Regulation 7

**History**
- Adopted as Assembly resolution A.862(20), 1997
- BLU Manual: guidance for terminals

**Applies to**
- Bulk carriers and terminals loading or unloading them

**Contents**
- Suitability of ships and terminals
- Ship and terminal agreement on responsibilities and information exchange
- Ship/shore **safety checklist**
- Cargo declaration; cargo information
- Loading and unloading plan, ballast changes, trim and stress
- Terminal: loading rates, stopping, communication

**Key documents**
- Ship/Shore Safety Checklist (Appendix 2)
- Loading or unloading plan agreed by master and terminal representative
- Cargo information from the shipper

**Key Point**
- Loading plan keeps hull stresses within limits; never exceed the loading instrument limits
- Master and terminal must agree rates and stop the operation if unsafe
