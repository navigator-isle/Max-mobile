**MASS Code (International Code of Safety for Maritime Autonomous Surface Ships)**

**Status**
- Adoption, voluntary status, effective date and mandatory timeline checked against the IMO press briefing (MSC 111, May 2026)
- Resolution number and chapter structure not found; to be verified

**What is it**
- First global framework for autonomous and remotely controlled ships
- Goal-based: design, approval and operation

**History**
- Adopted at MSC 111, 13-22 May 2026
- Non-mandatory; effective 1 Jul 2026
- Mandatory version planned: adopted by 1 Jul 2030, in force 1 Jan 2032

**Applies to**
- Cargo ships covered by SOLAS Chapter I
- Autonomous and remotely controlled ships must still comply with SOLAS and other mandatory IMO instruments

**Focus areas**
- Remote operations and Remote Operations Centres
- Navigation and connectivity
- Fire safety and search and rescue
- Risk assessment and system design
- Cybersecurity

**Key Point**
- Master keeps overall responsibility for the ship at all times, even when not on board
- Human oversight stays in place
