**INF Code (International Code for the Safe Carriage of Packaged Irradiated Nuclear Fuel, Plutonium and High-Level Radioactive Wastes on Board Ships)**

**Status**
- Checked against the IMO resolution text (MSC.88(71))
- Corrected from the first draft: classes, chapters, certificate name

**What is INF**
- Special requirements for ships carrying irradiated nuclear fuel, plutonium and high-level radioactive wastes in packages
- Mandatory under SOLAS Chapter VII Part D

**History**
- Adopted 27 May 1999, resolution MSC.88(71)
- SOLAS VII amendments by MSC.87(71)
- Mandatory from 1 Jan 2001

**Ship classes (by total aggregate activity carried)**
- INF 1: below 4,000 TBq
- INF 2: irradiated nuclear fuel or high-level waste below 2 × 10⁶ TBq; plutonium below 2 × 10⁵ TBq
- INF 3: no maximum aggregate activity

**Chapters**
- 1 General (definitions, INF classes, application, survey and certification)
- 2 Damage stability
- 3 Fire safety measures
- 4 Temperature control of cargo spaces
- 5 Structural considerations
- 6 Cargo securing arrangements
- 7 Electrical power supplies
- 8 Radiological protection
- 9 Management and training
- 10 Shipboard emergency plan
- 11 Notification of incidents involving INF cargo
- Appendix: certificate form

**Key requirements**
- Certificate: International Certificate of Fitness for the Carriage of INF Cargo (flag State or RO), after an initial survey
- Passenger ships may not carry cargo that needs an INF 3 ship
- Class INF 1: stability standard set by the Administration; INF 2 and 3 meet SOLAS damage stability standards
- Cargo space average temperature not above 55 °C; ventilation or refrigeration independent of other spaces, essential items duplicated
- Securing against accelerations: 1.5 g longitudinal and transverse, 1.0 g vertical up, 2.0 g vertical down
- INF 2 and 3: alternative power source able to run essential cooling and flooding systems and emergency services for at least 36 hours
- Fire safety: INF 2 and 3 need water fire-extinguishing, fixed machinery space extinguishing, cargo space cooling, detection and alarm
- Every INF ship carries an Administration-approved shipboard emergency plan
- Releases or probable releases are reported under SOLAS Regulation VII/7-1

**Key Point**
- Know the three ship classes and the 36-hour power and 55 °C limits
