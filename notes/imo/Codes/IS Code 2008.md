**2008 IS Code — International Code on Intact Stability, 2008**

**What is the IS Code**
- Intact stability criteria and guidance so ships do not capsize in normal operation
- **Part A** mandatory (through SOLAS II-1 and the 1988 Load Lines Protocol); **Part B** recommendations and guidance
- Basis for the ship's stability booklet and loading instrument checks

**History**
- Replaced the Code on Intact Stability A.749(18) (1993) and older A.167/A.562 (weather criterion)
- Adopted 4 Dec 2008, resolution MSC.267(85) (Part A mandatory via MSC.269(85) SOLAS and MSC.270(85) LL amendments)
- Mandatory from 1 Jul 2010
- Amended several times since (e.g. 2015–2016 criteria for ships engaged in anchor handling, lifting and towing)
- Second-generation intact stability criteria: Interim Guidelines MSC.1/Circ.1627 (2020) — vulnerability checks for parametric roll, pure loss of stability, dead ship, excessive acceleration, surf-riding/broaching (voluntary)

**Applies to**
- Ships and other marine vehicles of 24 m in length and over: cargo ships, cargo ships with timber deck cargo, grain ships, passenger ships, fishing vessels, special purpose ships, offshore supply vessels, MODUs, pontoons, containerships

---

**Part A — Mandatory criteria**
- **Ch 1 General**: purpose, definitions, dynamic stability phenomena in waves
- **Ch 2 General criteria (2.2) — righting lever (GZ) curve, after free-surface correction**
  - Area under GZ up to 30°: **≥ 0.055 m·rad**
  - Area up to 40° (or downflooding angle φf if less): **≥ 0.09 m·rad**
  - Area between 30° and 40° (or φf): **≥ 0.03 m·rad**
  - GZ at 30° or more: **≥ 0.20 m**
  - Maximum GZ at an angle **≥ 25°** (preferably above 30°)
  - Initial metacentric height GM₀: **≥ 0.15 m**
- **2.3 Severe wind and rolling (weather) criterion**
  - Steady wind heeling lever lw1 (wind pressure 504 Pa); angle of heel under steady wind φ0 **≤ 16° or 80% of deck-edge immersion angle**, whichever is less
  - Ship rolls to windward by φ1 (calculated from tables), then a gust lever lw2 = 1.5 × lw1
  - Area "b" (reserve) must be **≥ area "a"** (heeling energy)
- **Ch 3 Special criteria for certain types of ships**
  - 3.1 Passenger ships: heel from crowding of passengers to one side and from turning ≤ 10°
  - 3.2 Oil tankers ≥ 5,000 DWT: MARPOL Annex I Reg 27 criteria
  - 3.3 Cargo ships carrying timber deck cargo: area up to 40° ≥ 0.08 m·rad; max GZ ≥ 0.25 m; GM ≥ 0.10 m throughout the voyage (allowing for water absorption and icing)
  - 3.4 Grain ships: International Grain Code
  - 3.5 High-speed craft: HSC Code

**Part B — Recommendations and guidelines**
- Ch 2 Design criteria: fishing vessels, pontoons, containerships > 100 m, offshore supply vessels, special purpose ships, MODUs, anchor handling, lifting, towing
- Ch 3 Preparing stability information: free surface, permanent ballast, standard loading conditions, stability booklet, timber deck cargo operations
- Ch 4 Stability instruments (loading computers)
- Ch 5 **Operational provisions against capsizing**: precautions in heavy weather, ship handling, avoid resonance with waves (see MSC.1/Circ.1228)
- Ch 6 Icing considerations · Ch 7 Watertight and weathertight integrity (hatches, doors, freeing ports)
- Ch 8 Determination of lightship parameters (inclining test, lightweight check)

**Key Point**
- Every departure (and worst voyage) condition must meet the 2.2 GZ criteria and 2.3 weather criterion — check with the approved loading computer
- Free-surface correction is part of GM and GZ — slack tanks reduce stability
- Timber deck cargo, grain and passenger ships have extra criteria
