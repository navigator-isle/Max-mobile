**2008 IS Code (International Code on Intact Stability)**

**Status**
- Written from knowledge; the IMO resolution (MSC.267(85)) could not be opened during checking
- Treat criteria values as standard but still to be verified against the Code

**What is the IS Code**
- Intact stability criteria for ships, with rules on stability information and operation
- Part A is mandatory under SOLAS, Load Lines and MARPOL; Part B is recommendatory

**History**
- Adopted 4 Dec 2008, resolution MSC.267(85)
- In force 1 Jul 2010

**Applies to**
- Ships and other marine vehicles of 24 m and above

**Parts**
- Part A Mandatory criteria
  - Chapter 1 General
  - Chapter 2 General criteria
  - Chapter 3 Design criteria applicable to certain types of ships
- Part B Recommendations for certain types of ships and additional guidelines
  - includes operational provisions against capsizing, stability calculations, inclining test and lightweight check

**Main criteria (Part A, 2.2)**
- GZ area up to 30°: at least 0.055 m·rad
- GZ area up to 40° (or flooding angle if less): at least 0.09 m·rad
- GZ area between 30° and 40°: at least 0.03 m·rad
- GZ at 30° or more: at least 0.20 m
- Maximum GZ at an angle of at least 25°
- Initial GM: at least 0.15 m

**Weather criterion**
- Severe wind and rolling criterion applies to ships of 24 m and above

**Key Point**
- The loading computer and stability booklet must show these criteria are met in every loading condition
