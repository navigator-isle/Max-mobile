**HNS 2010 (Hazardous and Noxious Substances Convention, as amended by the 2010 Protocol)**

**Status**
- Entry into force, ratifications, scope, cap and certificate checked against the IMO press briefing (2026)
- Tier details and the oil-like split written from knowledge; to be verified

**What is HNS**
- International Convention on Liability and Compensation for Damage in Connection with the Carriage of Hazardous and Noxious Substances by Sea
- Original Convention 1996; 2010 Protocol made it workable

**History**
- Adopted 3 May 1996; Protocol adopted 30 Apr 2010
- Conditions for entry into force met on 29 May 2026 (12 States; cargo contribution threshold met)
- In force 29 Nov 2027 (18 months later)

**Applies to**
- Damage from more than 2,000 substances carried by sea (chemicals, oils, acids, fertilizers, alcohols, LNG, LPG)
- Loss of life, personal injury, property damage, economic loss, clean-up and environmental reinstatement

**Key points**
- Two tiers: strict liability of the shipowner, then the HNS Fund
- Total compensation capped at 250 million SDR per incident
- HNS Fund financed by contributions from receivers of HNS cargo in Parties
- Compulsory insurance and an HNS certificate of insurance on board (about 65,000 ships expected to need one)
- Complements CLC and Fund (oil) and the Bunkers Convention

**Key Point**
- Check the certificate list from late 2027; ships trading to Party States will need the HNS certificate
