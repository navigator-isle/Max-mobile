**OPRC 1990 (Oil Pollution Preparedness, Response and Co-operation)**

**Status**
- Draft written from knowledge; to be checked against the IMO text

**What is OPRC**
- International Convention on Oil Pollution Preparedness, Response and Co-operation, 1990
- Framework for national and international readiness for major oil pollution incidents
- OPRC-HNS Protocol 2000 covers hazardous and noxious substances

**History**
- Adopted 30 Nov 1990, after the Exxon Valdez (1989)
- In force 13 May 1995
- OPRC-HNS Protocol adopted 15 Mar 2000; in force 14 Jun 2007

**Applies to**
- Ships, offshore units, seaports and oil handling facilities of Parties

**Key points**
- Ships carry a **Shipboard Oil Pollution Emergency Plan (SOPEP)**; this is also required under MARPOL Annex I Reg 37
- Tankers of 150 GT and above and other ships of 400 GT and above
- Report oil pollution incidents to the nearest coastal State
- Parties set up national contingency plans and stockpile response equipment
- Ships under the HNS Protocol carry a Shipboard Marine Pollution Emergency Plan (SMPEP) for noxious liquid substances

**Key Point**
- SOPEP or SMPEP on board and exercised; see MARPOL Annex I and II
