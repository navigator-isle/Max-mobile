**Salvage 1989 (International Convention on Salvage)**

**Status**
- Draft written from knowledge; to be checked against the IMO text

**What is Salvage 1989**
- Sets rules for salvage operations, rewards and duties, and adds environmental protection

**History**
- Adopted 28 Apr 1989
- In force 14 Jul 1996
- Replaced the 1910 Brussels Salvage Convention

**Applies to**
- Salvage operations in the waters of a Party or in court proceedings there

**Key points**
- **No cure, no pay** remains the base; reward set by value saved, skill, risk, time
- **Special compensation** (Art. 14) for salvors preventing environmental damage, even when the ship is not saved
- Duties of the master and owner: co-operate, take care, accept salvors' help
- Duties of salvors: carry out work with due care, prevent damage to the environment
- Master may conclude salvage contracts for the owner and cargo

**Key Point**
- Lloyd's Open Form (LOF) is the common contract using these rules
