**COLREG 1972 (Collision Regulations)**

**Status**
- Draft written from knowledge; to be checked against the IMO text and latest amendments

**What is COLREG**
- Convention on the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea, 1972
- Rules of the road for all vessels on the high seas and connected waters

**History**
- Adopted 20 Oct 1972
- In force 15 Jul 1977
- Replaced the 1960 Regulations
- Amended by IMO Assembly resolutions (e.g. 1981, 1987, 1989, 1993, 2001, 2007)

**Applies to**
- All vessels upon the high seas and in connected waters navigable by seagoing vessels
- Local rules apply in harbours, rivers and lakes, but must follow COLREG as closely as possible
