**Hong Kong Convention 2009 — International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships**

**What is the HKC**
- IMO convention for safe, environmentally sound ship recycling: covers the ship (design, operation, preparation for recycling) and the recycling yard
- Ship: Inventory of Hazardous Materials (IHM) all its life · surveys and certificates
- Yard: Ship Recycling Facility (SRF) authorized by its State · Ship Recycling Facility Plan (SRFP) · ship-specific Ship Recycling Plan (SRP)
- Convention (21 Articles) + Annex (Regulations, Ch 1–4) + 2 Appendices of hazardous materials + IMO guidelines

**History**
- Adopted 15 May 2009, Hong Kong diplomatic conference
- Entry-into-force conditions: 15 States · 40% of world tonnage · recycling capacity ≥3% of their tonnage
- Bangladesh and Liberia ratified 26 Jun 2023 → conditions met
- In force 26 Jun 2025
- New ship = building contract on/after 26 Jun 2025, or (no contract) keel laid on/after 26 Dec 2025, or delivery on/after 26 Dec 2027
- MEPC 80 (2023): amendments to Appendices 1 and 2 adding cybutryne (matching the AFS Convention)

**Applies to**
- Ships flying a Party flag and ships in Party ports
- Ship Recycling Facilities under a Party's jurisdiction
- Survey and certificate requirements: ships ≥500 GT on international voyages
- Not covered: warships, naval auxiliaries, State ships on non-commercial service, ships <500 GT, ships operating only in own waters (Party should still act consistently)

**Articles (main)**
- Art 4 Control of ship recycling: Parties enforce on ships and yards
- Art 5 Survey and certification of ships
- Art 6 Authorization of Ship Recycling Facilities
- Art 8 Inspection of ships in port (PSC): limited to a valid certificate; detailed inspection on clear grounds
- Art 9 Detection of violations · Art 10 Violations: sanctions by flag or yard State · Art 11 Undue delay (compensation)
- Art 12 Communication of information (authorized yards, ROs) · Art 18 Amendments (tacit acceptance)

**Annex Ch 1 — General**
- Reg 1 Definitions (hazardous material, IHM, SRF, ship recycling, competent authority)
- Reg 2 General applicability · Reg 3 Relationship with other standards

**Annex Ch 2 — Requirements for Ships**

*Part A — Design, construction, operation, maintenance*
- Reg 4 Controls of hazardous materials (Appendix 1 — prohibited or restricted):
  - Asbestos: banned in all new installations
  - Ozone-depleting substances: banned on ships built ≥2009 (new installations)
  - PCBs: banned
  - Anti-fouling systems with organotin (TBT) or cybutryne: not applied (as AFS Convention)
- Reg 5 Inventory of Hazardous Materials (IHM):
  - Part I: materials in the ship's structure and equipment (Appendix 1 and 2) — new ships from delivery
  - Existing ships: Part I within 5 years of entry into force or before going for recycling, whichever is earlier (by 26 Jun 2030)
  - Part I kept up to date during the ship's life (new equipment, repairs)
  - Part II operationally generated wastes · Part III stores — prepared only before recycling
- Reg 6 Amending Appendices 1–2 · Reg 7 Exchange of information

*Part B — Preparation for ship recycling*
- Reg 8 General: recycle only at an authorized yard · minimize cargo residues, fuel, wastes before arrival
- Reg 9 Ship Recycling Plan: made by the yard, specific to each ship, before recycling
- Reg 10 Surveys:
  - Initial survey (before the ship enters service / IHM certificate first issued)
  - Renewal survey: interval ≤5 years
  - Additional survey: after a change, replacement or significant repair affecting the IHM
  - Final survey: before recycling — checks IHM Parts I–III, SRP, yard authorization
- Reg 11 Issue and endorsement of certificates:
  - International Certificate on Inventory of Hazardous Materials: after initial or renewal survey · validity ≤5 years
  - International Ready for Recycling Certificate (IRRC): after final survey · validity ≤3 months
  - Non-Party flag: Statement of Compliance (same contents)
- Reg 12 Issue by another Party · Reg 13 Form of certificates (Appendices 3–4) · Reg 14 Duration and validity

**Annex Ch 3 — Requirements for Ship Recycling Facilities**
- Reg 15 Controls on SRFs: authorized by competent authority · only recycle ships that meet the Convention
- Reg 16 Authorization: Document of Authorization to conduct Ship Recycling (DASR) · ≤5 years
- Reg 17 General requirements: management systems, training, emergency plans
- Reg 18 Ship Recycling Facility Plan (SRFP) · Reg 19 Prevention of adverse effects to health and environment
- Reg 20 Safe and environmentally sound management of hazardous materials
- Reg 21 Emergency preparedness · Reg 22 Worker safety and training (PPE)
- Reg 23 Reporting on incidents, accidents, occupational diseases and chronic effects

**Annex Ch 4 — Reporting Requirements**
- Reg 24 Initial notification and reporting · Reg 25 Reporting upon completion (Statement of Completion → Administration)

**Appendices**
- Appendix 1 — Controls (prohibited/restricted): asbestos, ozone-depleting substances, PCBs, anti-fouling compounds (organotin, cybutryne)
- Appendix 2 — Minimum list for the IHM: Appendix 1 items + cadmium, hexavalent chromium, lead, mercury, PBBs, PBDEs, PCNs, radioactive substances, certain short-chain chlorinated paraffins
- Appendices 3–7 — Certificate and report forms (IHM certificate, IRRC, DASR, recycling start, Statement of Completion)

**IMO guidelines (MEPC)**
- MEPC.379(80) — 2023 Guidelines for the development of the IHM
- MEPC.196(62) Ship Recycling Plan · MEPC.210(63) safe recycling (yards) · MEPC.211(63) authorization of SRFs
- MEPC.222(64) Survey and certification · MEPC.223(64) Inspection of ships (PSC)

**EU Ship Recycling Regulation (EU 1257/2013) — on top of HKC**
- EU-flag ships ≥500 GT: recycle only at yards on the European List (EU-approved)
- IHM required: EU ships and non-EU ships calling at EU ports (since 31 Dec 2020)
- Stricter than HKC on yards (e.g., impermeable floors, no beaching in practice) · EU IHM includes PFOS and HBCDD
- HKC certificate does not replace EU SRR compliance

**Key Point**
- Onboard: keep IHM Part I updated with every new or changed equipment · ask for Material Declarations (MD) and Supplier Declarations of Conformity (SDoC) from suppliers
- No asbestos on board: check spare parts (gaskets, insulation) before installing
- PSC checks: valid IHM certificate (or Statement of Compliance) and an up-to-date IHM Part I
- Before sale for recycling: final survey + IRRC (3 months) + yard with DASR
