**LC 1972 and London Protocol 1996 (Dumping at Sea)**

**Status**
- Entry-into-force dates checked against IMO status of treaties
- Other details written from knowledge, still to be verified

**What is LC / LP**
- Convention on the Prevention of Marine Pollution by Dumping of Wastes and Other Matter, 1972
- 1996 Protocol modernizes it: reverse list, only listed materials may be dumped

**History**
- LC 1972: in force 30 Aug 1975
- LP 1996: in force 24 Mar 2006

**Applies to**
- Deliberate disposal of wastes at sea from ships, aircraft, platforms
- Not normal operational discharges; those fall under MARPOL

**Key points**
- LC: prohibited list (Annex I) and special-permit list (Annex II)
- LP: ban on all dumping except waste on the reverse list (Annex 1): dredged material, sewage sludge, fish waste, vessels and platforms, inert geological material, organic natural material, bulky items
- Incineration at sea prohibited; export of wastes for dumping prohibited
- 2006 amendment: allows CO₂ sequestration in sub-seabed geological formations

**Key Point**
- Dumping needs a permit from the national authority; ships' garbage is handled by MARPOL Annex V
