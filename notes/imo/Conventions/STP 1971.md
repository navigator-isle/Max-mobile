# STP 1971 — Special Trade Passenger Ships Agreement (and 1973 Space Protocol)

## At a glance
| Item | STP Agreement 1971 | Space Requirements Protocol 1973 |
|---|---|---|
| Adopted | 6 Oct 1971 | 13 Jul 1973, London |
| In force | 2 Jan 1974 | 2 Jun 1977 |
| Parties (IMO table, 5 Sep 2023) | 18 | 17 |
| Entry into force rule | 6 months after 3 governments sign without reservation or deposit acceptance or accession (at least 2 with special trade ships or nationals carried) | Same rule, for governments party to the 1971 Agreement |
| Languages | English and French equally authentic | English and French equally authentic |

![[diagram:stp-figures|Key figures of the 1973 Space Rules]]

> **Key Point:** The 1971 Agreement sets the general safety rules. The 1973 Protocol adds the space rules (headroom, deck area, bunks, ventilation, a space certificate) for passengers carried in special trades.

## What is STP 1971
- Sets minimum safety rules for **special trade passenger ships**, such as pilgrim ships, carrying large numbers of unberthed passengers, especially in the Indian Ocean area
- Made after the 1931 **Simla Rules** became outdated. Among its parties the Agreement replaces and abrogates the Simla Rules (Art IV(b)).
- Its annexed Rules modify chapters II and III of the **1960 SOLAS Convention** (IMO)
- The Agreement and its Rules together are the general rules for the special trades referred to in SOLAS 1960 (Art IV(a))

## STP 1971 — Articles
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| Preamble | Parties to SOLAS 1960 recognize its chapters II and III could be modified for passenger ships carrying large numbers of unberthed passengers in special trades |
| I General obligations | Give effect to the Agreement and its Rules, an integral part of it |
| II Application | Passenger ships in the special trades registered in party countries. Such ships comply with the Convention's requirements for passenger ships, as modified by the Rules. Parties will consider applying later Convention revisions. |
| III Communication of information | Deposit with IMO: laws and regulations, specimens of certificates, list of non-governmental agencies authorized to act |
| IV Prior conventions | Replaces the Simla Rules, 1931 among parties |
| V Signature, acceptance, accession | Open for signature three months, then for accession. Open to SOLAS 1960 parties. |
| VI Entry into force | Six months after three governments, at least two with special trade ships or nationals carried in them · later acceptances: the later of entry into force or three months after deposit |
| VII Amendments | Unanimous acceptance, or a conference: adopted by two-thirds present and voting, in force 12 months after two-thirds accept. For acceptance by all, silence for 12 months counts as acceptance. |
| VIII Denunciation | After 5 years from entry into force for that government · effective 1 year after receipt |
| IX–XI | Territories · deposit and registration · languages |

## 1973 Protocol — Space requirements
- Technical rules on the space for carrying passengers, developed with bodies including the WHO (IMO)
- Applies to passenger ships in special trades registered in states party to SOLAS 1960, the 1971 Agreement and the Protocol (Art II)
- The Rules apply to new and existing ships, with relaxations for existing ships in Part III (Rule 3)
- An Administration may exempt a ship for a single voyage in exceptional circumstances, except from the Part IV health rules (Rule 4)

### Part II — Space rules
| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 9 Unsuitable spaces | No passengers where clear headroom is **less than 1.90 m (6 ft 3 in)** · not forward of the collision bulkhead · not on lower between decks within 10% of the ship's length from the forward perpendicular · not on unsheathed weather decks |
| 10 Number permitted | Limited by the Agreement, the bunk count (voyages of 72 hours or more) and airing space |
| 11 Spaces without bunks | Area per passenger from the scale in Appendix I · airing space on between decks not less than **0.37 m² (4 sq ft)** per passenger · for voyages of 24 hours or more, passengers in a space no more than the gross volume divided by **3.06 m³ (108 cu ft)** |
| 12 Deductions | 5% overall for baggage, plus deductions near stairways, lavatories, water taps, lifeboat areas and hatchways |
| 13 Spaces with bunks | Required for voyages of **72 hours or more** · bunks at least **1.90 m × 0.70 m** · passageways at least 0.70 m wide · bunks kept 0.90 m from hatches, 0.60 m from the ship's side frames and 0.75 m from stairways · the 3.06 m³ limit also applies |
| 15 Stairways | At least 0.05 m of aggregate width for every five passengers, minimum width 0.75 m |
| 16 Lighting | Required in all passenger spaces, day and night |
| 17 Ventilation | Trunked mechanical ventilation giving **at least 10 fresh air changes per hour**, or an approved air-conditioning system |
| 18 Awnings | Weather protection on exposed passenger decks |
| 19 Cooking | Passengers may not cook food on board |
| 20 Hatchways | Guarded to at least 0.90 m when open |
| 21 Obstruction | Passenger spaces, including airing spaces, kept free of cargo |

### Part III — Existing ships (Rule 22)
- Relaxed per-person areas of 0.9 m² and 1.12 m² for certain fair-weather voyages of 24 to 48 hours
- Bunks of 1.80 m length allowed in some cases

### Part IV — Health (Rule 23)
- Ships comply with the International Health Regulations (1969)

### Space certificate (Rules 5–8)
- **Special Trade Passenger Ship Space Certificate** issued after inspection
- Valid for **not more than twelve months**, in the model form in Appendix II
- Certificate or certified copy posted in a prominent place on board
- Certificates issued by one party are accepted by other parties
- Privileges under the Rules need a valid certificate

## Key terms
- **Season of fair weather / season of foul weather:** defined in Rule 2 (used for the space scales)
- **Upper and lower between deck, gross volume of a space:** defined in Rule 2
- The text speaks of spaces "fitted with bunks" and "not fitted with bunks"

## Not included (not verified against the original text)
- The per-passenger deck area table (Appendix I) and the Rules annexed to the 1971 Agreement
- Definitions of "special trade" and "special trade passenger ship" (not in the text checked)
- Current standing: IMO's treaty table (printed 5 Sep 2023) lists the dates and party counts only, with no note on amendment or replacement
- The 1960 SOLAS chapters II and III modified by the Rules, as later superseded by SOLAS 1974
- Practical figures on pilgrim trades and modern use of the Agreement
