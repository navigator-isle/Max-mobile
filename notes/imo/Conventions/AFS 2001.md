**AFS 2001 (Anti-Fouling Systems Convention)**

**Status**
- Draft written from knowledge; to be checked against the IMO text

**What is AFS**
- International Convention on the Control of Harmful Anti-fouling Systems on Ships, 2001
- Bans harmful organotin compounds (TBT) in hull anti-fouling paints

**History**
- Adopted 5 Oct 2001
- In force 17 Sep 2008
- 2021 amendment: control of cybutryne, in force 1 Jan 2023

**Applies to**
- Ships entitled to fly the flag of a Party, and ships operating under a Party's authority
- Fixed and floating platforms, FSUs and FPSOs

**Structure of the Annexes**
- Annex 1: controls on anti-fouling systems
  - organotin compounds acting as biocides: not applied after 1 Jan 2003, not present on hulls after 1 Jan 2008
  - cybutryne: not applied from 1 Jan 2023; on hulls, removed or overcoated by the first survey after that date, within the stated limit
- Annex 2: process for amending controls
- Annex 3: documents for initial proposal
- Annex 4: surveys and certification
- Annex 5: Declaration on Anti-fouling System (Form)

**Certificates**
- International Anti-fouling System Certificate: ships of 400 GT and above on international voyages
- Declaration on Anti-fouling System: ships of 24 m and above but under 400 GT
- Surveys: initial before the ship enters service or before the certificate is first issued; after any change or replacement of the system

**Key Point**
- Check the hull coating record, AFS certificate or declaration, and cybutryne status at the next dry dock
