**What is Intervention 1969**
- International Convention Relating to Intervention on the High Seas in Cases of Oil Pollution Casualties, 1969
- Coastal States may act on the high seas against a casualty that threatens grave and imminent oil pollution

**History**
- Adopted 29 Nov 1969, after the Torrey Canyon (1967)
- In force 6 May 1975
- 1973 Protocol (other substances): in force 30 Mar 1983

**Applies to**
- Coastal State measures beyond territorial waters after a maritime casualty

**Key points**
- Measures must be proportionate and necessary
- Consult the flag State and other affected States first, except in extreme urgency
- Compensation if the measures go beyond what was reasonably needed
- 1973 Protocol extends to substances other than oil listed or dangerous to health
