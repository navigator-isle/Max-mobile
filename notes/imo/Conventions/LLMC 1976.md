**LLMC 1976 (Limitation of Liability for Maritime Claims)**

**Status**
- Dates (1976, 1996 Protocol, 2012 amendment) match what I know; limit figures from memory, to be verified

**What is LLMC**
- Convention on Limitation of Liability for Maritime Claims, 1976
- Lets shipowners, salvors and insurers limit liability for certain claims, based on ship's GT

**History**
- Adopted 19 Nov 1976
- In force 1 Dec 1986
- 1996 Protocol: higher limits; in force 13 May 2004
- 2012 limits raised by amendment, in force 8 Jun 2015

**Applies to**
- Claims for loss of life, personal injury, property loss, delay, with separate limits for passenger claims

**Limits (2012 amendment, to verify against the IMO text)**
- Loss of life and personal injury: 3.02 million SDR up to 2,000 GT, increasing by tonnage band
- Other claims (property): 1.51 million SDR up to 2,000 GT, increasing by tonnage band
- Passenger claims: 175,000 SDR multiplied by the number of passengers the ship is certified to carry

**Key points**
- Two funds: personal injury and property claims
- Right to limit is lost only for personal act or omission done with intent or recklessly
- Does not cover oil pollution (CLC), HNS, nuclear damage, wreck removal if covered elsewhere

**Key Point**
- Underpins P&I cover limits and other liability conventions that refer to it
