**FAL 1965 — Convention on Facilitation of International Maritime Traffic**

**What is FAL**
- Simplifies and standardizes the formalities, documents and procedures for the arrival, stay and departure of ships, persons and cargo in ports
- Standard IMO forms (FAL 1–7) that public authorities must accept, and limits on what extra they may ask
- Since 2024: a **Maritime Single Window** for electronic exchange of all port clearance data

**History**
- Adopted 9 Apr 1965 (London) · in force 5 Mar 1967
- Annex amended many times; revised and restructured Annex (FAL.12(40)) in force 1 Jan 2018
- Electronic exchange of information mandatory from 8 Apr 2019 (Standard 1.3bis)
- **Maritime Single Window mandatory from 1 Jan 2024** (FAL.14(46), adopted 2022)

**Applies to**
- Public authorities of Parties (customs, immigration, health, port authorities) dealing with ships on international voyages
- Not: warships and pleasure yachts

---

**Annex — Standards and Recommended Practices**
- **Section 1 Definitions and general provisions**: electronic data exchange, single window, single submission of data, no unnecessary formalities
- **Section 2 Arrival, stay and departure of the ship**: documents public authorities may require (General Declaration, Cargo Declaration, Ship's Stores, Crew's Effects, Crew List, Passenger List, Dangerous Goods, Maritime Declaration of Health, security information, waste information); clearance on departure
- **Section 3 Arrival and departure of persons**: passports and visas, crew shore leave and identity documents (ILO C185), crew changes, measures for persons rescued at sea
- **Section 4 Stowaways**: shipowner and State duties, return of stowaways, no penalty on the ship when measures were reasonable; IMO revised guidelines on stowaways
- **Section 5 Arrival, stay and departure of cargo and other articles**: customs clearance, containers and pallets, unaccompanied baggage
- **Section 6 Public health and quarantine** (incl. sanitary and veterinary measures; IHR 2005)
- **Section 7 Miscellaneous provisions**: bonds, services at ports, emergency assistance, persons rescued at sea, stowaways
- Appendix: the standard FAL forms

**Standard IMO FAL forms**
- FAL 1 General Declaration
- FAL 2 Cargo Declaration
- FAL 3 Ship's Stores Declaration
- FAL 4 Crew's Effects Declaration
- FAL 5 Crew List
- FAL 6 Passenger List
- FAL 7 Dangerous Goods (manifest)
- Plus: Maritime Declaration of Health (IHR), security-related information (SOLAS XI-2/9), advance waste notification (MARPOL)

**Key Point**
- Ports must accept the IMO FAL forms and (since 1 Jan 2024) take them electronically through a single window
- Master/agent sends FAL data before arrival; keep crew list and stores list accurate — discrepancies delay clearance
- Stowaways: follow FAL Section 4 and the company's procedure; report to the next port and flag
