**SAR 1979 — International Convention on Maritime Search and Rescue**

**What is SAR**
- A worldwide search and rescue plan: the oceans are divided into SAR regions, each with a responsible State, Rescue Coordination Centre (RCC) and SAR services
- Persons in distress at sea are helped without regard to nationality, status or circumstances
- Practical procedures in the IAMSAR Manual (IMO/ICAO)

**History**
- Adopted 27 Apr 1979 (Hamburg)
- In force 22 Jun 1985
- Revised Annex 1998 (MSC.70(69)) · in force 1 Jan 2000 — clearer duties, regional co-operation, links to GMDSS
- 2004 amendments (MSC.155(78)) · in force 1 Jul 2006 — **place of safety**: the State responsible for the SAR region must make sure survivors are delivered to a place of safety (with SOLAS V/33 amendments)
- Global SAR plan agreed at Fremantle 1998: world divided into 13 large SAR areas

**Applies to**
- Parties, for their SAR regions and services
- Ship masters through SOLAS V/33 (duty to assist) and UNCLOS Art 98

---

**Annex**
- **Chapter 1 Terms and definitions**: search and rescue region, RCC, rescue sub-centre (RSC), SAR unit, distress phases, place of safety
- **Chapter 2 Organization and co-ordination**
  - Each Party sets up SAR services and RCCs; delimits SAR regions by agreement with neighbours
  - Co-ordination with aeronautical SAR (ICAO)
- **Chapter 3 Co-operation between States**: entry of rescue units into another State's territory; agreements; co-operation with ship owners and masters
- **Chapter 4 Operating procedures**
  - **Emergency phases**: INCERFA (uncertainty), ALERFA (alert), DETRESFA (distress)
  - Initiating and co-ordinating SAR operations; On-Scene Co-ordinator (OSC); ending and suspending operations
- **Chapter 5 Ship reporting systems**: voluntary ship reporting (e.g. AMVER) to know which ships are near a distress

**IAMSAR Manual (3 volumes)**
- Vol I Organization and Management
- Vol II Mission Co-ordination
- **Vol III Mobile Facilities** — must be carried on board ships (SOLAS V/21); on-scene actions, OSC duties, search patterns (expanding square, sector search, parallel track), signals, rescuing persons, helicopter operations

**Master's duties (SOLAS V/33 with SAR)**
- Proceed with all speed to assist persons in distress when informed; log the reason if unable to go
- Follow RCC instructions; act as On-Scene Co-ordinator if asked
- Treat survivors humanely; disembark them at a place of safety arranged by the responsible State

**Key Point**
- IAMSAR Vol III on the bridge and its checklists practised in drills — PSC checks it
- The State of the SAR region co-ordinates and arranges the place of safety; the master renders assistance
- Distress phases: INCERFA → ALERFA → DETRESFA
