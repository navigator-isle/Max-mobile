**SAR 1979 (International Convention on Maritime Search and Rescue)**

**Status**
- Draft written from knowledge; to be checked against the IMO text

**What is SAR**
- Global system of search and rescue regions, with Parties responsible for rescue in their own region
- Backed by the IAMSAR Manual

**History**
- Adopted 27 Apr 1979
- In force 22 Jun 1985
- 1998 amendments: in force 1 Jan 2000, plus later updates

**Applies to**
- Parties for their SAR regions; masters under SOLAS Reg V/33 and UNCLOS

**Annex chapters**
- Chapter 1 Terms and definitions
- Chapter 2 Organization and coordination
- Chapter 3 Cooperation between States
- Chapter 4 Preparatory measures
- Chapter 5 Operating procedures
- Chapter 6 Ship reporting systems

**Key points**
- Rescue Coordination Centres (RCCs) and Rescue Sub-Centres
- Masters must render assistance; survivors delivered to a place of safety
- IAMSAR Manual: Vol I Organization, Vol II Mission Co-ordination, Vol III Mobile Facilities

**Key Point**
- Ship's SAR drills and the IAMSAR Vol III on board are checked by PSC
