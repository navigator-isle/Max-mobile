**SUA 1988 (Suppression of Unlawful Acts Against the Safety of Maritime Navigation)**

**Status**
- Entry-into-force dates checked against IMO status of treaties
- Other details written from knowledge, still to be verified

**What is SUA**
- Convention for the Suppression of Unlawful Acts against the Safety of Maritime Navigation, 1988
- Makes seizing a ship, violence on board, destroying or damaging a ship an offence to be punished or extradited

**History**
- Adopted 10 Mar 1988, after the Achille Lauro (1985)
- SUA 1988 and SUA PROT 1988 (fixed platforms): in force 1 Mar 1992
- 2005 Protocols: SUA 2005 and SUA PROT 2005, in force 28 Jul 2010

**Applies to**
- Ships navigating beyond the territorial sea of one State, and fixed platforms on the continental shelf

**Key points**
- Offences: seizing a ship, acts of violence, placing a destructive device, destroying or damaging navigation facilities, false information
- States must establish jurisdiction, take offenders into custody, and prosecute or extradite
- 2005 Protocol adds: using a ship as a weapon, transporting explosives or radioactive material, WMD, transporting terrorists, and boarding procedures on suspicion

**Link to ISPS**
- ISPS Code (SOLAS Ch. XI-2) is the practical security system for ships and ports

**Key Point**
- Master may deliver an offender to a Party's authorities with evidence
