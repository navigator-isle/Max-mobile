**STCW-F 1995 — International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Fishing Vessel Personnel**

**What is STCW-F**
- First binding international standards for the training, certification and watchkeeping of crews on seagoing fishing vessels
- Sets minimum competence for skippers, officers, engineers and radio operators, and basic safety training for all fishing crew
- Separate from STCW 1978 (merchant ships) — fishing crews follow STCW-F

**History**
- Adopted 7 Jul 1995 (London)
- In force 29 Sep 2012 (12 months after the 15th Party)
- Comprehensive review 2019–2024: revised Annex (MSC.561(108)) and a new mandatory STCW-F Code (MSC.562(108)), both in force 1 Jan 2026
- Works with the Cape Town Agreement 2012 (vessel safety) and the ILO Work in Fishing Convention 2007 (C188)

**Applies to**
- Personnel on seagoing fishing vessels of Parties
- Most certification rules apply to vessels of 24 m in length and over; engine rules by propulsion power (750 kW and over)
- Not: fishing vessels on inland waters, pleasure craft, fish-processing ships with no fishing

---

**Convention (Articles)**
- Art 1 General obligations · Art 2 Definitions · Art 3 Application
- Art 4 Communication of information to IMO · Art 5 Other treaties · Art 6 Certificates
- Art 7 Transitional provisions · Art 8 Dispensation · Art 9 Equivalents
- Art 10 Control (port State control of foreign fishing vessels) · Art 11 Promotion of technical co-operation
- Art 12 Amendments (tacit acceptance) · Art 13–19 Signature, entry into force, denunciation, depositary, languages

**Annex (revised, in force 1 Jan 2026)**
- Chapter I General provisions: definitions, certificates and endorsements, medical fitness, revalidation, control procedures, national provisions
- Chapter II Certification of skippers, officers, engineer officers and radio operators
  - Skipper and officer in charge of a navigational watch on fishing vessels of 24 m and over in unlimited waters
  - Skipper and officer of fishing vessels of 24 m and over in limited waters
  - Chief and second engineer officers of fishing vessels with main propulsion of 750 kW or more
  - GMDSS radio operators
- Chapter III Basic safety training for all fishing vessel personnel
- Chapter IV Watchkeeping: basic principles for navigational watches on fishing vessels (lookout, handover, fishing operations, navigation with pilot on board)

**STCW-F Code (new, mandatory from 1 Jan 2026)**
- Part A: minimum standards of competence in tables (like the STCW Code) — knowledge, understanding, proficiency, methods of demonstrating competence
- Covers: navigation, stability (including effect of catch and fishing gear), fishing-gear handling safety, fire, survival, first aid, medical care, environment protection (MARPOL), human relationships
- Part B: recommended guidance

**Basic safety training (all crew)**
- Personal survival techniques · fire prevention and fire-fighting · elementary first aid · personal safety and social responsibilities
- Before being assigned any shipboard duty

**Key Point**
- Fishing skippers and officers need STCW-F certificates, not STCW merchant certificates — check the flag's national rules
- Since 1 Jan 2026 the competence tables in the STCW-F Code apply, like STCW for merchant officers
- Port States can inspect certificates of foreign fishing vessel crews (Art 10)
