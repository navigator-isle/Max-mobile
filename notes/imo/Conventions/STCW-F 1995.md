**STCW-F 1995 (Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Fishing Vessel Personnel)**

**Status**
- Written from knowledge; to be checked against the IMO text

**What is it**
- First international standards for training and certification of fishing vessel crew

**History**
- Adopted 7 Jul 1995
- In force 29 Sep 2012

**Applies to**
- Crew of seagoing fishing vessels of 24 m and over

**Structure**
- Convention articles plus Annex
  - Chapter I General provisions
  - Chapter II Certification of skippers and officers
  - Chapter III Basic safety training (reference)
  - Chapter IV Watchkeeping
- Details of the standards are in the Annex rather than the articles

**Key Point**
- Applies to fishing vessels, not merchant ships; merchant seafarers follow STCW 1978
