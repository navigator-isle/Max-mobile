**Cape Town Agreement 2012 (Fishing Vessel Safety)**

**Status**
- Entry into force, ratification count and scope checked against the IMO press briefing (2026)
- Certificate and survey detail not confirmed; to be verified

**What is it**
- Agreement on the Implementation of the Provisions of the 1993 Torremolinos Protocol relating to the 1977 Torremolinos International Convention for the Safety of Fishing Vessels
- Sets binding international safety rules for fishing vessels

**History**
- Adopted 11 Oct 2012 in Cape Town
- Needed 22 States with at least 3,600 qualifying fishing vessels; threshold met (28 States, 3,754 vessels, latest Argentina, Feb 2026)
- In force Feb 2027 (12 months later)

**Applies to**
- Fishing vessels of 24 m in length and over (over 45,000 vessels per FAO)

**Covers**
- Design, construction, equipment, inspection
- Stability and seaworthiness
- Machinery and electrical installations
- Life-saving appliances, fire protection, communications

**Key Point**
- Flag States enforce through national law; port States may inspect foreign fishing vessels
- Works with STCW-F 1995 (crew training) and the ILO Work in Fishing Convention
