**BWM 2004 — International Convention for the Control and Management of Ships' Ballast Water and Sediments**

**What is BWM**
- Stops the spread of harmful aquatic organisms and pathogens (e.g. zebra mussel, cholera) carried in ships' ballast water and sediments
- Ships must manage ballast to the **D-1 exchange** or **D-2 performance** standard, keep a plan, a record book and (≥ 400 GT) a certificate
- Since 8 Sep 2024 practically all ships must meet D-2 (an approved treatment system, BWMS)

**History**
- IMO guidelines 1993/1997 (A.868(20)) before the Convention
- Adopted 13 Feb 2004 · in force **8 Sep 2017** (35% of world tonnage reached 8 Sep 2016)
- MEPC.297(72): revised D-2 implementation schedule (Reg B-3) · in force 13 Oct 2019
- MEPC.296(72)/MEPC.300(72): **BWMS Code** mandatory for type approval of systems installed from 28 Oct 2020
- MEPC.325(75): **commissioning testing** of BWMS (sample and analyse at installation) · in force 1 Jun 2022
- Experience-building phase 2017–2024 → review of the Convention; MEPC 84 (Apr–May 2026) finalised amendments so ships must show the BWMS is working, maintained and meeting D-2 (not just installed)

**Applies to**
- Ships of a Party designed or constructed to carry ballast water, on international voyages
- Not: ships not designed to carry ballast water; ships only in waters of one Party (unless that Party decides); warships; permanent ballast in sealed tanks

---

**Section A — General provisions**
- A-1 Definitions · A-2 General applicability · A-3 **Exceptions** (safety of the ship or saving life at sea; accidental discharge from damage; avoiding pollution; same location uptake and discharge) · A-4 **Exemptions** (between specified ports, by risk assessment, up to 5 years) · A-5 Equivalent compliance (pleasure craft < 50 m, max 8 m³ ballast)

**Section B — Management and control requirements for ships**
- B-1 **Ballast Water Management Plan** (approved, ship-specific: procedures, safety, sediment disposal, BWM officer)
- B-2 **Ballast Water Record Book** (each operation recorded; kept 2 years on board, 3 more years under company control)
- B-3 Implementation schedule → D-2 at the first IOPP renewal survey after 8 Sep 2019 (or second if the first was 8 Sep 2014 – 8 Sep 2017), all by 8 Sep 2024
- B-4 **Ballast water exchange** (D-1, where still allowed): at least 200 nm from nearest land in ≥ 200 m depth; if impossible, at least 50 nm and ≥ 200 m; not required if it would endanger the ship or deviate the voyage
- B-5 Sediment management (to reception facilities or at sea per the plan) · B-6 Duties of officers and crew (familiar with the plan)

**Section C — Special requirements in certain areas**: Parties may add stricter measures (with IMO notice)

**Section D — Standards**
- **D-1 Exchange standard**: ≥ 95% volumetric exchange; pumping-through 3 × tank volume counts as meeting it
- **D-2 Performance standard** (discharge):
  - < 10 viable organisms per m³ ≥ 50 µm
  - < 10 viable organisms per mL 10–50 µm
  - Toxicogenic *Vibrio cholerae* < 1 cfu per 100 mL (or 1 cfu per g zooplankton)
  - *Escherichia coli* < 250 cfu per 100 mL
  - Intestinal enterococci < 100 cfu per 100 mL
- D-3 Approval of BWMS (BWMS Code; Active Substances approved by IMO, G9 procedure) · D-4 Prototype technologies · D-5 Review of standards

**Section E — Survey and certification**
- Ships ≥ 400 GT: initial, annual, intermediate, renewal surveys; **International Ballast Water Management Certificate** (up to 5 years)
- Ships < 400 GT: Administration may set appropriate measures

**On board (practical)**
- Run the BWMS as in its manual; record any bypass or failure; contingency measures (MEPC guidance) if the BWMS fails — inform the port State before arrival
- PSC may take indicative or detailed samples at discharge points

**Key Point**
- D-2 with an approved, commissioned BWMS is now the rule; D-1 exchange only as a contingency where the port allows
- Plan, record book and certificate on board; every uptake, treatment, discharge and failure recorded
- PSC checks documents, crew familiarity, BWMS operation, and may sample
