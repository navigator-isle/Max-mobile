**BWM 2004 — International Convention for the Control and Management of Ships' Ballast Water and Sediments**

**What is BWM**
- Aim (Art 2): prevent, minimize and ultimately eliminate the transfer of harmful aquatic organisms and pathogens
- Ships manage ballast water to the **D-1 exchange** or **D-2 performance** standard, carry a Ballast Water Management Plan and a Ballast Water Record Book; ships ≥ 400 GT also carry the International Ballast Water Management Certificate
- D-2 deadline: all ships by 8 Sep 2024 at the latest (see B-3)

**History**
- Adopted 13 Feb 2004 · in force **8 Sep 2017** (Art 18: 12 months after 30 States representing 35% of world merchant tonnage; the 35% was reached 8 Sep 2016)
- MEPC.296(72) (amends A-1 and D-3) + MEPC.300(72): **BWMS Code** made mandatory (adopted MEPC 72, Apr 2018; in force Oct 2019); it superseded the G8 Guidelines from Oct 2019
- MEPC.297(72): revised D-2 implementation schedule (Reg B-3) · MEPC.299(72): amended E-1 and E-5 (endorsement of additional surveys)
- MEPC.325(75): **commissioning testing** made mandatory (amends Reg E-1 and the Appendix I certificate form) · adopted Nov 2020 · in force 1 Jun 2022
- MEPC.369(80): new Appendix II (Ballast Water Record Book form) · MEPC.383(81): amends A-1 and B-2 to allow electronic record books · IMO listed them as expected in force Feb 2025 and 1 Oct 2025
- Experience-building phase set up at MEPC 71 (Jul 2017, MEPC.290(71)) → data analysis report MEPC 78 (2022) → Convention Review Plan MEPC 80 (2023) → list of provisions to amend endorsed MEPC 81 (Mar 2024)
- MEPC 84 (27 Apr–1 May 2026): approved the draft amendments from the Correspondence Group on the review of the Convention; entry into force to be decided at MEPC 85 so they take effect together with the BWMS Code. Direction: ships must prove the BWMS is working, maintained and meeting D-2, not just installed and type-approved
- MEPC 84 also dealt with the 2026 G4 Guidelines (ballast water management and BWM Plans; they replace the earlier G4) and approved a unified interpretation of Reg D-3 (to go into BWM.2/Circ.66): systems approved under the 2016 G8 Guidelines are treated as equal to the BWMS Code and may still be installed

**Structure**
- Annex Regulations (A-, B-, D-, E- series) + Appendix I (form of the International BWM Certificate) + Appendix II (form of the Ballast Water Record Book)
- Key IMO guidelines: G1 sediment reception facilities · G2 sampling · G3 equivalent compliance · G4 BWM plans (MEPC.127(53), amended; 2026 revision at MEPC 84) · G5 reception facilities · G6 ballast water exchange (2017, MEPC.288(71)) · G7 risk assessment under A-4 (2017, MEPC.289(71)) · G8 BWMS approval (2016, MEPC.279(70); superseded by the BWMS Code Oct 2019) · G9 active substances (MEPC.169(57)) · G10 prototype technology (MEPC.140(54)) · G11 exchange design and construction standards (MEPC.149(55)) · G12 sediment control design (MEPC.209(63)) · G13 additional measures incl. emergency situations (MEPC.161(56)) · G14 designation of exchange areas (MEPC.151(55))

**Section A — General provisions**
- A-1 Definitions
- A-3 **Exceptions** — the standards do not apply to: uptake or discharge needed for the ship's safety in an emergency · accidental discharge or ingress from damage to the ship or equipment · operations to avoid or minimise pollution from the ship · uptake and discharge of the same ballast water on the high seas · discharge at the place where all the ballast water originated, if no unmanaged water from elsewhere was mixed in
- A-4 **Exemptions** — for ships on voyages between specified locations (or in a defined area); max 5 years; only if the ballast water is not mixed outside the named locations; based on a risk assessment under G7
- A-5 Equivalent compliance — for pleasure craft used solely for recreation or competition, and craft used primarily for search and rescue (G3: craft < 50 m long with max ballast capacity of 8 m³)

**Section B — Management and control requirements for ships**
- B-1 **Ballast Water Management Plan** (specific to the ship)
- B-2 **Ballast Water Record Book** (kept on board at least 2 years, then under company control at least 3 more years)
- B-3 Implementation schedule: D-2 is tied to the IOPP renewal survey (renewal survey after 8 Sep 2019 → D-2 by that survey, depending on the previous survey); ships without an IOPP renewal survey: date set by the flag State, not later than 8 Sep 2024; ships built on or after 8 Sep 2017: D-2 from delivery
- B-4 **Ballast water exchange** (D-1): at least 200 nm from the nearest land and in water ≥ 200 m deep; if impossible, at least 50 nm from land and ≥ 200 m deep

**Section D — Standards**
- **D-1 Exchange standard**: at least 95% volumetric exchange (typically by pumping through 3 × the tank volume)
- **D-2 Performance standard** (discharge):
  - < 10 viable organisms per m³ of size ≥ 50 µm
  - < 10 viable organisms per mL of size 10 to < 50 µm
  - Toxicogenic *Vibrio cholerae* (O1 and O139) < 1 cfu per 100 mL (or < 1 cfu per g wet weight of zooplankton)
  - *Escherichia coli* < 250 cfu per 100 mL
  - Intestinal enterococci < 100 cfu per 100 mL
- D-3 Approval of BWMS: BWMS Code; systems using Active Substances need approval by IMO (G9 procedure)

**Section E — Survey and certification**
- Ships ≥ 400 GT: **International Ballast Water Management Certificate**, valid up to 5 years (shows the expiry date)
- Ships < 400 GT: no survey/certificate requirement, but they must carry a Ballast Water Management Plan and a Record Book
- Art 9: port State control officers may verify that the ship has a valid certificate

**Key Point**
- Plan and Record Book on board; certificate if ≥ 400 GT
- D-2 is the standard; D-1 exchange only follows the B-4 distance and depth rules
- PSC can verify the certificate
