**Bunkers 2001 (Bunker Oil Pollution Damage Convention)**

**Status**
- Corrected: threshold is over 1,000 GT, not 1,000 and above
- Rest written from knowledge; to be checked against the IMO text

**What is Bunkers 2001**
- International Convention on Civil Liability for Bunker Oil Pollution Damage, 2001
- Liability and compensation for pollution from bunker oil of any ship (non-tanker ships)

**History**
- Adopted 23 Mar 2001
- In force 21 Nov 2008

**Applies to**
- Pollution damage in the territory, territorial sea and EEZ of a Party
- Registered owner of a ship over 1,000 GT needs compulsory insurance or financial security

**Key points**
- Registered owner, bareboat charterer, manager and operator can be liable (shipowner, defined widely)
- Strict liability, with limited defences
- Limit under the applicable national or international regime (for example LLMC)
- Certificate of insurance or financial security carried on board; direct action against the insurer

**Key Point**
- Ships over 1,000 GT carry the Bunkers Convention certificate; PSC and port entry checks look for it
