**Athens Convention 2002 (PAL) — Athens Convention relating to the Carriage of Passengers and their Luggage by Sea, 1974, as amended by the 2002 Protocol**

**What is Athens 2002**
- Liability of the carrier for death or personal injury of passengers and for loss of or damage to their luggage
- 2002 Protocol: much higher limits, strict liability for shipping incidents, and compulsory insurance with direct action against the insurer

**History**
- Athens Convention adopted 13 Dec 1974 · in force 28 Apr 1987
- Protocols of 1976 (SDR unit) and 1990 (never in force)
- 2002 Protocol adopted 1 Nov 2002 · in force 23 Apr 2014
- IMO Reservation and Guidelines (2006): lets States limit insurers' liability for terrorism and war risks (separate cover)
- EU: Regulation (EC) 392/2009 applies Athens 2002 also to many domestic ferries

**Applies to**
- International carriage of passengers by sea when the ship flies a Party's flag, the contract was made in a Party, or the place of departure or destination is in a Party
- Carriers (contracting and performing carriers)

---

**Liability for death and personal injury (per passenger, per incident)**
- **Shipping incident** (shipwreck, capsizing, collision, stranding, explosion, fire, defect in the ship):
  - Strict liability up to **250,000 SDR** — defences only for war/natural disaster or intentional third-party act
  - Above that, up to **400,000 SDR** total unless the carrier proves the incident happened without its fault or neglect
- **Non-shipping incident** (e.g. a fall on board): liability only for proven fault or neglect, up to 400,000 SDR
- States may set higher (or no) limits by national law

**Luggage and vehicles (per passenger, per carriage)**
- Cabin luggage: 2,250 SDR
- Vehicles including luggage in or on them: 12,700 SDR
- Other luggage: 3,375 SDR
- Valuables (money, jewellery) only if deposited with the carrier
- Carrier's deductible allowed: up to 330 SDR (vehicles) and 149 SDR (other luggage)

**Compulsory insurance (Art 4bis)**
- Ships licensed to carry more than 12 passengers
- Cover of at least 250,000 SDR per passenger per incident
- Certificate of insurance issued by the flag State (or a Party) and carried on board
- Claimants may sue the insurer directly

**Time limits**
- Action within 2 years (from disembarkation, or from when disembarkation should have taken place); suspension or interruption under the court's law, but never later than 5 years from disembarkation (or 3 years from when the claimant knew of the injury, if earlier)

**Key Point**
- Passenger ships carry the Athens (PAL) certificate of insurance — PSC checks it with the other liability certificates
- Strict liability up to 250,000 SDR per passenger for shipping incidents; up to 400,000 SDR unless the carrier proves no fault
- Report and document every passenger injury — claims can come up to 2 years later
