**Athens 2002 (Carriage of Passengers and their Luggage by Sea)**

**Status**
- Written from knowledge; limits and dates to be verified against the IMO text

**What is it**
- 2002 Protocol to the Athens Convention 1974; liability for death, personal injury and loss of luggage of passengers

**History**
- Convention adopted 1974; 2002 Protocol adopted 1 Nov 2002
- 2002 Protocol in force 23 Apr 2014

**Key points**
- Carrier strictly liable for shipping incidents (shipwreck, collision, stranding, explosion, fire, defect) up to 250,000 SDR per passenger per incident
- Fault-based liability for other incidents up to 400,000 SDR per passenger
- Compulsory insurance for ships carrying more than 12 passengers
- Certificate of insurance on board; direct action against the insurer
- IMO Reservation and Guidelines address war and terrorism risks for insurers

**Key Point**
- Passenger ship certificate list includes the Athens certificate
