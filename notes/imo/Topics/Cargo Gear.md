# Cargo Gear — Testing, examination, records and safe use of ship's lifting gear

## At a glance
| | |
|---|---|
| **Main source** | **ILO Convention No. 152**, Occupational Safety and Health (Dock Work), 1979, Articles 21 to 27, with the ILO **model register and certificates** (Nov 1985) |
| **What it covers** | **Lifting appliances** (cranes, derricks, winches) and **loose gear** (blocks, chains, hooks, rings, shackles, swivels, slings, lifting beams, spreaders) |
| **Test** | By a competent person **before first use**, after **substantial alteration or repair**, and for ship's lifting appliances at least every **5 years** |
| **Thorough examination** | At least every **12 months** by a competent person |
| **Records** | A **register** in the form the competent authority prescribes, with certificates, kept up to date |
| **Marking** | The **safe working load (SWL)** is marked on lifting appliances (except ship's derricks) and loose gear |

> **Key Point:** Never lift above the SWL. Use only gear that has a valid certificate and is entered in the register. A test load above the SWL is used only for a test under a competent person.

## Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lifting appliance** | Crane, derrick, winch and similar plant that lifts the cargo |
| **Loose gear** | Items that attach the load: blocks, chains, hooks, rings, shackles, swivels, slings, lifting beams, spreaders and frames |
| **SWL** | Safe working load. The load the item may lift in use |
| **Test load (proof load)** | The load above SWL used in the test |
| **Competent person** | The person or organization recognized by the competent authority to test, examine and certify |
| **Thorough examination** | A detailed visual examination, supplemented as needed |

## What the Convention requires (ILO 152, Part III)
| Article | Rule |
|---|---|
| **21 General** | Lifting appliances, loose gear and integral slings are **well designed**, **strong enough**, **maintained** and **used safely**. They are **not used above SWL**, except for tests directed by a competent person |
| **22 Testing** | A competent person tests **before first use** and **after a substantial alteration or repair**. **Ship's lifting appliances** are tested at least every **5 years**. Shore appliances follow the competent authority's schedule. Tested items are thoroughly examined and certified |
| **23 Examination** | Periodic thorough examination by a competent person at least every **12 months** |
| **24 Inspection** | Loose gear is **inspected before use** by a responsible person. **Expendable or disposable slings are not reused**. Pre-slung cargo slings are inspected as often as reasonably practicable |
| **25 Records** | Authenticated records of **SWL** and of the dates and results of tests and examinations. Routine sling inspections are recorded only if a **defect** is found. A **register** is kept in the form the competent authority prescribes |
| **26 Recognition** | Competent persons and organizations for ship equipment are recognized between Members. An appliance is not used if the competent authority lacks satisfactory certification or finds it unsafe |
| **27 Marking** | SWL marked on appliances (except ship's derricks) and loose gear. An appliance with several loads has a way for the operator to know the load that applies. **Ship's derricks** show the loads for **single purchase**, **lower cargo block** and **union purchase** in all block positions |

**Related articles**
- **28:** ships carry **rigging plans** for safe derrick rigging
- **30:** loads are slung or attached safely before lifting
- **38:** only persons **18 or over** with the aptitude and experience, or trainees under proper supervision, operate lifting or cargo-handling appliances
- **17, 18, 19, 20:** safe access to holds, hatch cover condition and operation, protection at openings, and work in holds
- **13:** machinery guards, and stopping machinery before dangerous maintenance with a restart prevented

## The ILO register and certificates (model forms, 1985)
| Form | What it records |
|---|---|
| **No. 1** Register of ship's lifting appliances and cargo handling gear | Ship identity. **Part I** thorough examinations of appliances and loose gear (certificate numbers, type of examination: initial, 12-monthly, five-yearly, repair or damage, other including heat treatment). **Part II** regular inspections of loose gear and defects found |
| **No. 2** Certificate of test and thorough examination of lifting appliances | Description, angle or radius of the test, test load, SWL at that angle or radius, the firm or competent person |
| **No. 2(U)** Derricks used in union purchase | Description, maximum triangle plate height or maximum angle between runners, test load, SWL(U), positions of preventer guy attachment |
| **No. 3** Loose gear | Distinguishing mark, description, number tested, date, test load, SWL, maker or supplier |
| **No. 4** Wire rope | Diameter, strands, wires per strand, core, lay, wire quality, sample test date, breaking load, SWL, intended use. Rope is tested to destruction by sample |

- The model replaced the forms issued under Convention No. 32. It is in English only. Authorities usually issue their own register, often with a translation beside the English
- **No new loose gear** is made of wrought iron. Heat treatment follows the manufacturer's instructions, satisfies the competent person and is **recorded in Part I**

## Test loads
**Lifting appliances (Form 2)**
| SWL | Test load |
|---|---|
| **Up to 20 t** | **25%** above SWL |
| **20 to 50 t** | **5 t** above SWL |
| **Over 50 t** | **10%** above SWL |
| **Hydraulic cranes** | In general not less than **10%** above SWL |

- **Derricks** are tested at the minimum angle to the horizontal the system was designed for, **generally 15°**, or a greater angle if agreed

**Loose gear (Form 3)**
| Item | SWL | Test load |
|---|---|---|
| **Single-sheave block** | Any | **4 × SWL** |
| **Multi-sheave block** | 25 t or less | **2 × SWL** |
| | Over 25 t to 160 t | **0.933 × SWL + 27** t |
| | Over 160 t | **1.1 × SWL** |
| **Chain, hook, ring, shackle, swivel** | 25 t or less | **2 × SWL** |
| | Over 25 t | **1.22 × SWL + 20** t |
| **Lifting beam, spreader, frame** | 10 t or less | **2 × SWL** |
| | Over 10 t to 160 t | **1.04 × SWL + 9.6** t |
| | Over 160 t | **1.1 × SWL** |

- Test loads are in tonnes. These are the ILO model instructions. A national authority may specify other requirements

## Examinations: summary
| What | When |
|---|---|
| **Test and thorough examination** | Before first use. After substantial alteration or renewal. After repair of any stress-bearing part |
| **Re-test of ship's lifting appliances** | At least every **5 years** |
| **Thorough examination of all gear** | At least every **12 months** |
| **Inspection of loose gear** | Before each use, by a responsible person |
| **Slings that are expendable or disposable** | Never reused |
