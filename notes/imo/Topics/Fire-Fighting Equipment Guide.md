# Fire-Fighting Equipment Guide — What is on board, how many, and how it is kept

## At a glance
| Group | Equipment | SOLAS II-2 | Technical standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water | Fire pumps, fire main, hydrants, hoses, nozzles, international shore connection | 10.2 | [[FSS Code]] chapters 2 and 12 |
| Portable | Extinguishers, spare charges, foam applicator | 10.3 | FSS Code chapter 4 |

![[diagram:ffe-main|The fire main]]

> **Key Point:** Two jets of water from two different hydrants must reach every part of the ship. Everything else on this list is there to back that up, or to protect the people using it.

## Fire pumps, hydrants, hoses (SOLAS II-2/10.2)
| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Fire pumps, number** | Passenger ≥4,000 GT: **three**, independently driven. Passenger under 4,000 GT: **two**. Cargo ≥1,000 GT: **two**. Cargo under 1,000 GT: **two** power-driven, one independently driven |
| **Pumps used** | Sanitary, ballast, bilge or general service pumps, if not normally used for oil |
| **Emergency fire pump** | Needed if one fire could disable all the pumps. Its power and sea connection are **outside the main pump spaces**. Its space is not next to a Category A or main pump space. Access by an airlock or remotely operated watertight door, with a second route |
| **Total capacity** | Passenger: **two-thirds** of the bilge pump quantity. Cargo: **four-thirds** of the bilge quantity of a same-size passenger ship, **capped at 180 m³/h** |
| **Each pump** | **≥80%** of the total divided by the minimum pump count, and **≥25 m³/h**. Each must deliver the two required jets |
| **Pressure at hydrants** (two pumps running) | Passenger ≥4,000 GT **0.40 N/mm²**. Passenger under 4,000 GT **0.30**. Cargo ≥6,000 GT **0.27**. Cargo under 6,000 GT **0.25** |
| **Fire main size** | For two pumps running together. A cargo ship's main need only carry **140 m³/h** |
| **Hydrants** | **Two jets** from different hydrants, one from a single length of hose, reach any normally accessible part, any empty cargo space and any ro-ro or vehicle space |
| **Isolating valves** | Outside the machinery space and accessible. A valve on each hydrant lets a hose be removed with the pumps running. **Relief valves** where pumps can exceed design pressure |
| **Ready water** | Cargo ships with a machinery space unattended or only one person on watch: water delivered immediately by **remote start** of a main pump from the bridge and fire control station, or by **permanent pressurization** |
| **International shore connection** | Ships **≥500 GT**, usable on either side. Dimensions in the [[FSS Code]] |

**Hoses and nozzles**
- **Length:** at least **10 m**. At most **15 m** in machinery spaces, **20 m** elsewhere and on open decks, **25 m** on open decks where the breadth is over 30 m
- **Couplings** fully interchangeable, unless each hydrant has its own hose and nozzle
- **Number, cargo ship ≥1,000 GT:** one for each **30 m** of ship length, plus one spare, **at least five**. Engine and boiler room hoses are not counted. Ships carrying dangerous goods add **three**
- **Number, cargo ship under 1,000 GT:** the same rule, at least **three**
- **Passenger ships:** at least one per hydrant, used only for fire-fighting or testing
- **Nozzle sizes:** **12, 16 and 19 mm**. 12 mm is enough in accommodation and service spaces. In machinery spaces and outside, the size that gives the most water from two jets, **not above 19 mm**
- **Nozzle type:** dual-purpose (spray and jet), with a shut-off

## Portable extinguishers (SOLAS II-2/10.3, FSS Code chapter 4)
- **Approved types** only, to IMO guidelines (FSS 1.2)
- **Size:** powder or CO₂ at least **5 kg**. Foam at least **9 L**. No portable unit above **23 kg**, and each at least as effective as a 9 L fluid extinguisher
- **Number:** suitable types in accommodation, service and control spaces. Ships **≥1,000 GT carry at least five**. One near the entrance of each space
- **No CO₂ extinguishers in accommodation.** Electrical and electronic spaces use non-conductive media that do not damage the equipment
- **Condition:** visible, quickly reachable, protected from weather and vibration, with a **used indicator**
- **Spare charges:** **100%** of the first **10** rechargeable extinguishers and **50%** of the rest, **maximum 60**. Recharging instructions on board. A non-rechargeable unit gets extra extinguishers instead
- **Recharging** only with the charge approved for that extinguisher
- **Portable foam applicator:** inductor nozzle on the fire main by a hose, a tank of at least **20 L** of foam liquid plus a spare tank, foam at least **1.5 m³/min**

## By space: what else is required
| Space | Required |
|---|---|
| **Machinery, oil-fired boiler or oil fuel unit** (Cat A) | Fixed system. A **portable foam applicator**. At least **two** portable foam extinguishers per firing space. A foam extinguisher of at least **135 L** per boiler room, with hose reels. **0.1 m³** of sand per firing space, with a shovel |
| **Machinery, internal combustion** (Cat A) | Fixed system. A portable foam applicator. Foam extinguishers of at least **45 L**, no point more than **10 m** walking distance from one, at least two in the space |
| **Steam turbines or enclosed steam engines** (≥375 kW, periodically unattended) | Fixed system. Foam extinguishers of at least **45 L** with the same 10 m rule |
| **Local application** | Passenger ≥500 GT and cargo ≥2,000 GT: a fixed water-based local system in Cat A spaces over **500 m³** |
| **Cargo spaces** | Cargo ships ≥2,000 GT (not ro-ro or vehicle spaces): fixed **CO₂ or inert gas**, or an equivalent. Dangerous goods ships: the same. Ore, coal and grain ships may be exempted, with steel hatch covers |
| **Paint lockers** | **CO₂** for **40%** of the gross volume, or dry powder at **0.5 kg/m³**, or water spray at **5 L/m²·min**. Operable from outside |
| **Galley deep-fat cooking** | Automatic or manual extinguishing system, primary and backup thermostats with a failure alarm, automatic power cut-off, galley alarm |
| **Tankers** | Deck foam system (FSS Code chapter 14). Cargo pump-room: fixed CO₂, high-expansion foam or water spray, from outside |
| **Passenger ships over 36 passengers** | Sprinklers, detection and alarm in control stations, accommodation and service spaces. Two water fog applicators per Cat A space |
- **Fixed systems allowed:** gas, high-expansion foam, pressure water-spray. **Halon 1211, 1301, 2402 and perfluorocarbons are banned.** Steam is not allowed except as a small extra in restricted areas
- **Storage** is behind the forward collision bulkhead, for no other purpose, with its own entrance. Below deck it must be within one deck of the open deck, with at least **6 air changes an hour** by mechanical exhaust
- Technical figures for each fixed system are in the [[FSS Code]]

## Personal protection
![[diagram:ffe-outfit|The fire-fighter's outfit]]

**Fire-fighter's outfit (FSS Code chapter 3)**
- **Protective clothing** against radiant heat, burns and steam scalds, with a water-resistant outer surface
- **Boots** of rubber or other non-conducting material. **Rigid helmet** with impact protection
- **Electric safety lamp** with at least **3 h** burning time, explosion-proof on tankers and in hazardous areas
- **Axe** with a high-voltage insulated handle
- **Breathing apparatus:** compressed air with at least **1,200 L** in the cylinders, or another type lasting at least **30 min**. Cylinders interchangeable
- **Lifeline:** fireproof, at least **30 m**, withstands **3.5 kN for 5 min**, snap-hook to the harness or a belt

**How many (II-2/10.10)**
| Ship | Outfits |
|---|---|
| **All ships** | At least **two** |
| **Tankers** | **Two more** |
| **Passenger ships** | For every **80 m** (or part) of the length of passenger and service spaces on the deck with the most, **two outfits** and **two sets of personal equipment**. Over 36 passengers: **two more outfits** in each main vertical zone |
| **Spare air charges** | **Two** per breathing apparatus. One per apparatus on a passenger ship of up to 36 passengers, or a cargo ship that can recharge cylinders. Passenger ship over 36: at least two |
- Stored where **easily accessible**, **permanently marked**, and **widely separated** if more than one
- Passenger ships over 36 passengers: one **water fog applicator** per pair of breathing apparatus, stored beside it

**Emergency escape breathing devices (II-2/13.3.4)**
- **Accommodation:** at least **two**, on every ship
- **Passenger ships:** at least **two in each main vertical zone**, and **two more** if over 36 passengers
- **Machinery spaces:** kept in easily visible places, reachable quickly at any time. Number and place consider the layout and the people who work there. They are shown on the **fire control plan**
- **Specification:** service time at least **10 min**. Flame-resistant hood or full face piece with a clear window. For **escape only**: not for fire-fighting, not for entering oxygen-deficient spaces, and not worn by fire-fighters
- **Marking:** maintenance needs, trademark, serial number, shelf life with the date of manufacture, approving authority. Training units are marked
- Spare devices are carried. Crew training in EEBD is part of on-board training

## Plans, manual and training (II-2/15)
- **Fire control plans:** general arrangement plans **permanently exhibited** for officers, showing for each deck the control stations, A-class and B-class sections, detection, alarm, sprinklers and extinguishing appliances, access routes, and the ventilation fan controls and damper positions. Alternatively a booklet, a copy for each officer and one always on board. Kept up to date
- A **duplicate set** is stored in a **prominently marked weathertight enclosure outside the deckhouse** for shore-side fire-fighters
- **Training manual:** one in each crew mess room and recreation room, or in each crew cabin. In the ship's working language, illustrated where possible. Covers fire safety, fire-fighting procedures, the ship's alarms, appliances, fire doors, dampers and escape
- **Drills** are recorded (III/19). Interval in [[SOLAS 1974]]

## Maintenance and testing
*Intervals from Malta Technical Notice SLS.6 Rev.4 (Feb 2025), which follows MSC.1/Circ.1432. Other flag States may differ.*

| Equipment | Weekly | Monthly | Annual | Longer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire main, hydrants, hoses, nozzles** | | Check. Run pumps, confirm pressure | Inspect, flow-test pumps, test hydrant valves, check relief valves | **Hoses:** a sample pressure-tested so all are tested within **5 years**, at maximum fire main pressure. Shore connection checked **quarterly** |
| **Fire-fighter's outfit** | | Lockers hold the full inventory, serviceable | | |
| **SCBA** | General check and cylinder gauges | | **Air quality** tested at an accredited laboratory. Masks and demand valves checked | **5-yearly:** cylinders tested at an approved shore station |
| **EEBD** | General check and gauges | | Per maker | Hydrostatic test at maker's interval, at a shore facility |
| **Portable extinguishers** | | | Serviced per the ship's schedule. Permanently pressurised units go ashore | **5-yearly:** discharge-test at least one of each type made in the same year. **10-yearly:** hydraulic test of extinguishers and cartridges |
| **Detection and alarm** | Panel lamp test | A sample of detectors and call points, so all are tested within **5 years** | Full test, including units that release extinguishing systems | |
| **Fire doors, dampers** | Panel indicators | | Remote release of doors and dampers | **Quarterly:** local operation of fire doors and dampers |
| **Sprinklers, water spray** | Panel, pumps, valves | Valve positions, tank levels, pump starts | Test with section valves, flow-test pumps | 5-yearly valve inspection. 10-yearly cylinder tests |
| **Foam systems** | | Valve positions | Test, sample concentrate | **Quarterly:** foam tank quantity. **5-yearly:** inspect control valves |
| **Other fixed gas** | Panel, valve positions | Gauges, leaks | Inspect | **Biennial:** weigh cylinders, refill below **95%**. **10-yearly:** test 10% of cylinders |

**Fixed CO₂ (MSC.1/Circ.1318/Rev.1)**
- **Monthly (at least every 30 days):** visual check for damage, stop valves closed, release controls accessible, cylinders secured, alarms in place
- **Annual:** protected-space boundaries, containers, piping and nozzles, manifold hoses, doors and signs
- **Biennial** (2 years ± 3 months for passenger ships, at each survey for cargo ships): weigh cylinders, **refill any below 90%** of nominal charge, blow-test piping, test the activating heads and alarms
- **10-yearly:** internally inspect and hydrostatically test at least **10%** of cylinders. If any fail, test **50%**, then all. **5-yearly:** inspect control valves internally

## Certificates and records
**For the ship**
- **Approved types:** extinguishers and equipment are approved under IMO guidelines (FSS Code 1.2)
- **Records** of inspection, maintenance and tests. Extinguisher **test dates** are stamped on CO₂ cylinders and tagged on other types
- **SCBA** air-quality test from an accredited laboratory (ISO/IEC 17025), each year
- **Foam concentrate** sample tests (MSC.1/Circ.1312 for low expansion, MSC/Circ.670 for high expansion)
- **EEBD** shelf life and serial number marked on each unit
- **Fire control plan** and **training manual** on board, drills recorded

**For people**
- **STCW A-VI/1-2, basic fire prevention and fire-fighting:** fire organization, location of appliances and escape routes, the fire triangle, ignition sources, fire and smoke detection, classes of fire and the right agent, and use of fire-fighting equipment and breathing apparatus
- **STCW A-VI/3, advanced fire-fighting:** control fire-fighting operations, organize and train fire parties, **inspect and service** detection and extinguishing systems, and investigate fire incidents
- Renewal intervals are in [[STCW 1978]]
