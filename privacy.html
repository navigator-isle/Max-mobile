Max — privacy

Max is a personal assistant app built for and used only by its owner, Mark Jun. It is not offered to the public.

What Max uses

  • Google Calendar and Tasks: to read the owner's schedule and add events, reminders and to-dos he asks for.
  • Gmail: to read and summarize the owner's email and to prepare drafts. An email is sent only after the owner explicitly confirms it.
  • Notes and files the owner gives Max, to answer his questions.

How data is handled

  • Data is used only to answer the owner's own requests. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Google access is stored encrypted on Max's private server (Cloudflare) and can be removed at any time from Max's settings ("Disconnect Google") or from the owner's Google Account permissions page.
  • Text sent to the AI model (Google Gemini) is used only to produce Max's reply.
  • Only the owner's Google account can sign in.

Max's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: the owner, through the Google account used for this app.
